Direction is normalized to unit length and controls the simulated truth only; estimators must infer it. Orientation matters when the noise differs by coordinate.

Profiles set each channel’s RMS noise SD to 1 and the true direction to x. Manual SDs keep their absolute values. Signal / noise means magnitude divided by the channel’s RMS component SD; fixed masses remain physical masses.

Repeated readings & noise calibration

Supplied SD / true SDxyz Force factor Acceleration factor

A factor of 0.75 understates the noise SD by 25%; 1.25 overstates it by 25%. All methods that use noise information receive the same supplied values.

Each trial receives an independent noise calibration. Its estimated standard deviations are treated as fixed during mass estimation; uncertainty in calibration is not included in the reported mass intervals.

Signal / noise settings describe a single reading. Repeats preserve the same true force and acceleration; their mean has noise SD divided by √(readings). This tests repeated readings at one fixed excitation.
