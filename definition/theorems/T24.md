## Statement

an exact zero reading has $H_i\equiv0$, so $E_i$ is constant for any $\Sigma_i$, any $k_i$

## Proof or support

**B06 derivation (Codex, 30 Oct).** This is the zero-vector case of the
noise-metric projection in T36. For any allowed $\Sigma\succ0$ and candidate
subspace, $G=C^\top\Sigma^{-1}C\succ0$. If the entire observed reading is zero,
$b=C^\top\Sigma^{-1}y=0$, so $H=b^\top G^{-1}b=0$ at every candidate slope.
Both derivatives of H vanish as well. The span energy is zero, hence $Q_+=0$
and ours30 assigns $g=1$; its per-reading log-factor $\ell=gH/2$ is identically zero.

Appending this reading leaves the other readings' own weights unchanged. It adds
zero to the series criterion, its curvature and its squared-score sum J, so it
leaves the best line and $\eta$ unchanged, including the stated fallbacks. The
unnormalized law is therefore multiplied by 1. Its normalizer, law, median and
intervals are unchanged. For an all-zero series the law is exactly the declared
starting law $\pi$, with median c for the half-Cauchy in `equation.md`.
This concerns the law and its present median point; the historical cloud readout
has a separate size functional (T29).
