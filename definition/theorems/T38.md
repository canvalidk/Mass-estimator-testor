# T38: The supremum of the expected positive-mass misfit is k − ½

## Setup

One reading, whitened: $x=Re_1+\xi$, $y=be_1+\zeta$, $\xi,\zeta\sim N(0,I_k)$ independent, $R,b\ge0$.
$A=\lVert x\rVert^2$, $B=\lVert y\rVert^2$, $C=x\cdot y$; $Q=\lambda_-$ of the Gram matrix (the sign-blind
misfit, ours29's); $Q_+=Q$ if $C>0$, else $\min(A,B)$ (T32). $f_k(R,b)=E[Q_+]$.

## Statement

1. $\sup_{R,b\ge0}E[Q_+]=k-\tfrac12$ for every $k\ge1$. It is **not attained** at any finite pair; it is
   approached as $R\to\infty$ with $b\to0$ (or $b=0$), and by the swap as $b\to\infty$ with $R\to0$.
2. $\sup_{R,b\ge0}E[Q]=k-1$, not attained for $k\ge2$; approached whenever $R^2+b^2\to\infty$. Both
   channels growing is **not** needed (the brief's "reached only as both channels grow" was wrong).
   For $k=1$, $Q\equiv0$.
3. At fixed total strength $L=\sqrt{R^2+b^2}$, $f_k(R,b)\le f_k(L,0)=f_k(0,L)$, strictly inside the
   quadrant: putting all the signal in one channel is the worst allocation.
4. On that boundary $f_k(L,0)=\tfrac12E[Q]+\tfrac12E[\min(A,B)]$, strictly increasing in $L$, so the
   supremum splits exactly as $\tfrac12(k-1)+\tfrac12k$: half the time the noise gets the sign right
   and the reading pays the $k-1$ codirectionality conditions, half the time it gets it wrong and pays
   the whole of the signal-free channel. That is the ruled reading of κ (R-2026-10-29-1: "$k-1$ for the
   codirectionality conditions (L8) and ½ for L1's sign condition"), now exact.
5. $f_k(R,b)=f_k(b,R)$.

## Proof

In O13 (link above), in three steps: (1) at fixed $L$, conditional on the Gram eigenvalues, the
principal eigenvector's line angle has density $\propto K(\theta-\alpha)$ with $K$ even, π-periodic and
decreasing in projective distance; $Q_+=\lambda_-+\Delta\sin^2\operatorname{dist}(\theta,[0,\pi/2])$, whose
superlevel sets are arcs centred at $3\pi/4$, so by the layer-cake identity $E[Q_+]$ is largest at
$\alpha\in\{0,\pi/2\}$; (2) on $b=0$, fitting $y$ on $x$'s nonnegative ray gives $Q_+\le B-(U\cdot y)_+^2$,
whose mean is $k-\tfrac12$, strictly larger on the event $C<0$, $A<B$; the limit along $(n,1/n)$ by
dominated convergence; (3) $Q=2AV/(A+W+V+\sqrt{(A-W-V)^2+4AW})<V\sim\chi^2_{k-1}$, with the limit as
$L\to\infty$, and monotonicity through the Poisson coupling of the noncentral χ².
