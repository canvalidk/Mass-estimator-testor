## Statement

Fisher-orthogonal under ours28's model; the reference prior on mass is uniform in $\theta$ (the half-Cauchy) for any size law

## Proof or support

### The ours28 calculation and its scope

The source is `../../docs/ours27/ours28_size_measure_jeffreys_check_2026-09-28.md`
§1. Take one independent force–acceleration noise pair with standard
deviations $\sigma_F,\sigma_a$, and write $s=\sigma_F/\sigma_a$ and
$\theta=\arctan(m/s)$. In whitened coordinates ours28's model per axis is
$$
 z=v u(\theta)+e,\qquad
 u=(\sin\theta,\cos\theta)^\top,\qquad
 v\sim N(0,\omega^2),\quad e\sim N(0,I_2),
$$
with $v$ and $e$ independent and $\omega>0$. Thus its marginal model is
$z\sim N(0,V)$, where $V=I_2+\omega^2uu^\top$.

Let $t=\partial_\theta u=(\cos\theta,-\sin\theta)^\top$. In the orthonormal
basis $(u,t)$, the three matrices are
$$
 V=\begin{pmatrix}1+\omega^2&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
 V_\theta=\omega^2\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
 V_\omega=\begin{pmatrix}2\omega&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
For a centered Gaussian covariance model,
$I_{ab}=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(V^{-1}V_aV^{-1}V_b)$. Substitution gives
$$
 I_{\theta\theta}=\frac{\omega^4}{1+\omega^2},\qquad
 I_{\omega\omega}=\frac{2\omega^2}{(1+\omega^2)^2},\qquad
 I_{\theta\omega}=0.
$$
So mass angle and size are Fisher-orthogonal. Both information blocks depend
only on $\omega$. At fixed $\omega>0$, the scalar Jeffreys density for
$\theta$ is therefore constant. With $\theta$ the parameter of interest,
the regular reference-prior construction also produces a constant in
$\theta$: eliminating the orthogonal nuisance and averaging its log
information produces a factor independent of $\theta$. Here "for any size
law" means a size treatment independent of $\theta$ for which that
construction is well-defined. A size law changes the information level;
it supplies no preferred angle in this rotational model. At $\omega=0$
the mass is unidentified, so the zero-information model itself does not
derive a reference density.

The existing named test uses the earlier **fixed-signal** model
$z\sim N(r u(\theta),I_2)$. Its mean derivatives are $rt$ and $u$, giving
$I_{(\theta,r)}=\operatorname{diag}(r^2,1)$. This independently checks
orthogonality and the constant-angle form in that model; it does not by
itself test the displayed ours28 marginal information. The separate
`test_ours28_gaussian_size_marginal_fisher_is_theta_omega_orthogonal` checks
that matrix using numerical covariance derivatives and the Gaussian trace
formula, for three positive sizes and several angles.

### The half-Cauchy member of the admissible family

For any declared $c>0$, take $\theta_c=\arctan(m/c)$ in $(0,\pi/2)$. A uniform
probability density $2/\pi$ in this coordinate transforms to
$$
 \pi_{\rm HC}(m)
 =\frac{2}{\pi}\frac{d\theta_c}{dm}
 =\frac{2c}{\pi(c^2+m^2)},\qquad m>0.
$$
The equation's $c/(c^2+m^2)$ is the same density up to its omitted constant.
Its CDF is $2\arctan(m/c)/\pi$, hence its median is $c$. For $S(m)=c^2/m$,
$$
 \pi_{\rm HC}(S(m))\,|S'(m)|=\pi_{\rm HC}(m),\qquad
 \theta_c(S(m))=\frac\pi2-\theta_c(m).
$$
Equivalently, in $x=\ln(m/c)$ its normalized density is
$g_{\rm HC}(x)=m\pi_{\rm HC}(m)=1/(\pi\cosh x)$, which is even and integrable.
This places it inside the family derived in O6. In the ours28 calculation
above the noise-defined angle is $\theta_c=\theta$ only when $c=s$.

L3, L4 and properness allow other even normalized log-mass densities.
They do not require uniformity in $\theta_c$; T7's counting the starting law
once also supplies no such shape condition. The reference-prior principle
is additional structure in the historical model, not a selection forced
by those three constraints. No member is selected here, and O6 remains open.

### Under the current working law

With L10's removal of the size law in ours29/ours30, the retained reference
form does not depend on a choice of size law. The finite-$\omega$ Fisher
calculation belongs to ours28's probability model. It does not derive a
reference prior afresh for ours30's tempered working likelihood, or force
the half-Cauchy as its unique admissible input. T36's parked question about
the induced measure on $L_i(m)$ is not addressed by this coordinate change.
