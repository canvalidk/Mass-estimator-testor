## Standard mathematics and scope

CDF composition is standard probability recalibration: see Kuleshov, Fenner
and Ermon (2018), [section 3.2](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/kuleshov18a/kuleshov18a.pdf).
Their recalibrator is learned from a separate calibration sample; no such
sample, exchangeability assumption or learning guarantee is imported here.
The operator below is derived from an explicit Gaussian benchmark. It is
a scalar alternative to changing an unnormalized likelihood or transporting
samples in a chosen physical coordinate.

The full Gaussian local benchmark is stronger than property N's direct
wording. The theorem sorts a mathematical class, not the definition's whole
admissible family. In particular, T43's leverage failure in estimating eta
does not disappear by replacing its global application.

## 1. A conditional uniqueness theorem

Consider a calibration operator T_eta that receives **only** a raw law P
and a scalar 0<eta<=1. On continuous strictly increasing full-support laws
on ordered open intervals, assume:

1. It commutes at the **law level** with every increasing homeomorphism.
2. A Gaussian parent N(mu,sigma^2) becomes N(mu,sigma^2/eta).

Then its output CDF is uniquely

$$F_{T_\eta(P)}(m)=H_\eta(F_P(m)),\qquad
H_\eta(u)=\Phi(\sqrt\eta\,\Phi^{-1}(u)).$$

Indeed, g=F_P^{-1} composed with Phi transports a standard normal to P.
Naturality forces T_eta(P)=g_*T_eta(N(0,1)). Applying the Gaussian requirement
and taking a CDF gives the displayed formula. Conversely that formula
satisfies both assumptions. Thus a normal-score coordinate is not freely
chosen inside **this class**: the raw law supplies it.

The extension H_eta(0)=0, H_eta(1)=1 is continuous and increasing, so
H_eta composed with any proper CDF is itself a proper CDF, including atomic
laws. It is continuous under weak convergence. The uniqueness argument above
concerns the stated continuous class; extending the class requires its own
regularity assumption or the explicit displayed construction.

Neither existing L23 nor L17 supplies the class reduction: L23 is a proposed
requirement on the **point**, and L17 constrains the readout. A law operator
may still use the separately declared prior, factor geometry or other allowed
experiment state. Those richer operators are not reduced to this one by the
proof. This is the precise unforced step, not a free numerical tuning constant.

## 2. Exact identities and the prior at null data

For every positive eta and kappa,

$$H_1(u)=u,\qquad H_\eta(H_\kappa(u))=H_{\eta\kappa}(u),\qquad
H_\eta(1-u)=1-H_\eta(u).$$

For generalized quantiles the output obeys

$$F_{T_\eta(P)}^{-1}(p)=F_P^{-1}\left[
\Phi\left(\frac{\Phi^{-1}(p)}{\sqrt\eta}\right)\right].$$

In particular its median is **exactly the raw median**, for every eta>0,
not merely asymptotically. All increasing reparameterizations commute with
the construction. The reflection identity also gives channel reciprocity,
using left limits in the CDF formula when atoms occur. Units and other
bookkeeping transformations are inherited when P and eta transform properly.

If an exact-zero reading changes neither raw P nor eta, it changes neither
output. Constant factors and all-zero/empty data use the current eta=1
fallback, so **any** declared proper prior is then returned unchanged.
This is different from requiring T_eta(pi)=pi for every prior and arbitrary
eta. For a continuous full-support prior, its CDF covers (0,1), so that
stronger requirement would force H_eta to be the identity. It is not a new
interpretation of Z or a requirement added here.

For continuous P with density p, the output density is

$$q(m)=\sqrt\eta\exp\left[\frac{1-\eta}{2}
\{\Phi^{-1}(F_P(m))\}^2\right]p(m).$$

The multiplier is a derivative in **probability rank**. No change to a
candidate mass plane, latent signal or declared instrument covariance is
made. Normalization of P happens **before** this operation; replacing its
density or likelihood by a power is a different map.

The eta=0 endpoint is not a universal proper-law extension. For a raw law
with full support on (0,infinity), H_eta(F_P(m)) tends to 1/2 at every finite
positive m as eta decreases to zero. Probability escapes to the two parameter
boundaries. The research reference therefore requires positive eta, as does
the current mathematical Bartlett ratio with its zero-curvature fallback;
it does not add a new zero-eta branch to the definition.

## 3. Concentration, median consistency and vanishing eta

Suppose raw laws P_n concentrate at a finite interior m0. For each fixed
neighbourhood, write t_n^- and t_n^+ for their lower and upper outside tail
probabilities. Symmetry gives the corrected tails exactly as H_eta_n(t_n^-)
and H_eta_n(t_n^+). Consequently if eta_n is bounded below by a positive
constant, the corrected laws also concentrate. This needs no separate
fitted centre or chosen mass coordinate.

For arbitrary positive eta_n, assuming each raw tail tends to zero, the
exact necessary and sufficient condition is

$$\sqrt{\eta_n}|\Phi^{-1}(t_n^\pm)|\longrightarrow\infty.$$

By the Gaussian tail asymptotic this is equivalent to

$$\eta_n\log(1/t_n^\pm)\longrightarrow\infty.$$

Zero tails stay zero and can be handled separately. In probability over
random data the same equivalences follow by the corresponding convergence
in probability. Polynomial raw amplification tails t_R~R^(-c), c>0,
therefore require eta_R*log(R) to diverge. Fixed positive limiting eta works;
eta of order 1/log(R) leaves nonzero outside probability.

For fixed eta the small-rank asymptotic is

$$H_\eta(t)\sim\eta^{-1/2}
[4\pi\log(1/t)]^{(\eta-1)/2}t^\eta\quad(t\downarrow0).$$

Thus a raw power-law survival tail of positive exponent c becomes one of
positive exponent eta*c, up to slowly varying factors. It remains proper.
There is no density-integrability threshold of the T39 global-power kind,
because the raw normalization has already been priced into t.

Every raw-median consistency statement transfers **regardless** of eta_n's
rate, since the median is unchanged. Conversely every raw-median failure
is preserved. In particular, applying the warp to unchanged ours31 cannot
repair T39's nu<1 single-reading median 1.8219669... at truth 2.

For T39's nu=10,d=3 opposed pair, the raw law concentrates and eta tends to
1/90. The warped law and its median therefore concentrate, whereas the
old powered law does not. This needs no additional experiment-scale a of
T40. It is an alternative global calibration class, not a contradiction
of T40's scalar-deviance-map obstruction.

## 4. Regular Gaussian calibration transfers, with a tail limitation

If a continuous raw law has a TV Gaussian local limit and eta_n stays in
[epsilon,1], its warped law has the correspondingly inflated Gaussian local
limit. To verify TV transfer, truncate raw/reference ranks to [a,1-a].
On that interval H_eta' is bounded and uniformly continuous, uniformly in
eta>=epsilon. Raw TV convergence controls both the density difference and
the uniform CDF difference. Integrating the displayed density formula gives
vanishing interior TV error; endpoint contributions are bounded by the
corresponding H_eta-transformed tail ranks, which vanish uniformly as a
decreases to zero. No unproved moving-extreme-quantile approximation is used.

### The repeated fixed-signal regime

For T39's isotropic iid **Gaussian world**, fixed nonzero signal length,
finite fixed nu>0 and positive constant factor exponent a, the raw criterion
-a log(nu+K(theta)) has the true angle as its separated population maximum.
Let C=E[-ell'']>0 and J=E[(ell')^2]>0, given by T39 with that a.
Gaussian moments bound the first three derivative envelopes on the angular
circle. Uniform laws of large numbers, the iid score CLT and Taylor's theorem
give

$$\sqrt N(\widehat\theta-\theta_0)\Rightarrow N(0,J/C^2),\qquad
\mathcal H_N/N\to C,\quad J_N/N\to J.$$

For a fixed proper prior density positive and continuous at interior truth,
the raw law has a TV local limit N(0,1) in coordinate
$\sqrt{NC}(\theta-\widehat\theta)$. Briefly, Taylor's remainder on bounded
root-N coordinates is O_p(N^(-1/2)); uniform positive curvature near truth
gives a quadratic tail bound there, and separated population loss gives
exponentially small posterior mass outside that neighbourhood. These bounds
give tightness as well as local expansion, hence normalized TV convergence.

With eta_N=min(1,H_N/J_N), the limit eta_inf=min(1,C/J) is positive. The
warped central p interval has endpoints

$$\widehat\theta\ \pm z_p/\sqrt{\eta_NNC}+o_p(N^{-1/2}).$$

Its asymptotic sampling coverage is

$$2\Phi\left(z_p\sqrt{\frac C{\eta_\infty J}}\right)-1\ \ge p.$$

It equals p when the cap is inactive and is conservative otherwise. This
holds for both the current raw residual exponent and T48's corrected one
under the stated regular Gaussian regime. It is not an arbitrary-leverage
or full-type theorem about estimating eta; T43 remains load-bearing.

### Plain local BvM is insufficient when eta vanishes

In a raw standardized coordinate let

$$P_N=(1-e^{-N})N(0,1)+e^{-N}N(N^2,1),\qquad\eta_N=1/N.$$

Its TV error from N(0,1) is at most e^(-N). But at cutoff N^2/2 its raw
survival is asymptotic to e^(-N), and the corrected survival tends to
Phi(-sqrt(2))=0.0786496035... . This mass escapes even after sandwich
rescaling by sqrt(eta_N), whereas the Gaussian reference has no such mass.
Embedding this coordinate as x=N*log(m/m0) gives local raw curvature N^2
and eta_N*curvature=N tending to infinity, yet a nonvanishing corrected
remote tail. Thus neither ordinary raw BvM nor divergent corrected local
curvature alone proves concentration or calibrated BvM when eta vanishes.
This is a continuous proper-law counterexample, not a claim that ours32
produces these particular mixtures. Moving-tail control is the missing
condition; median consistency remains unaffected.
