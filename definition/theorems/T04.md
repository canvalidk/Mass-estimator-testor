**Scope of the measurement bridge (Codex, 28 Oct).** This theorem concerns one
supplied joint magnitude law and the full-domain composition assumptions of the
6 Sept proof. It does not assert that fitting summed measurements equals pushing
forward a fit to all the original measurements. The latter can retain an
informative contrast even when covariance and reference measure are propagated
correctly. `../../problems/O2.md`, "Physical composition and measurement coarsening",
derives the distinction and its exceptional ancillary-contrast case;
`tests/test_composition_bridge.py` checks the Gaussian factorization and an
all-positive-noise counterexample. Deriving the full readout-law domain from the
current measurement type remains an obligation. Neither this scope check nor the
existing Monte Carlo readout test proves that bridge for ours29.
