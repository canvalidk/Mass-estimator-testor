## Scope (28 Oct, Claude, provisional)

True of laws of joined sizes $(X,Y)$ drawn from one posterior, where the lump's force is
$X_1+X_2$. Data never supply that: the lump is a separate measurement with its own noise, and
on data the ratio of means fails lumping about as much as the law's median (0.27% against
0.27% at strong signal, 21% against 23% at weak; `tests/test_point_composition.py`). With L5
and L14 sorted as exact-value commitments (O2, 28 Oct), this theorem no longer constrains the
point.
