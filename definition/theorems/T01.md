## Statement

$\sigma=0$: one mass, no mass, or every mass; "undefined" only at exact $0/0$. **Pairing form (10 Oct):** compute two totals, $m_1=\mathbf a'(\mathbf F)=\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf a/\lvert\mathbf a\rvert^2$ and $m_2=1/\mathbf F'(\mathbf a)=\lvert\mathbf F\rvert^2/(\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf a)$, and their ratio $P=m_1/m_2=\cos^2\theta$. One mass iff $P=1$ and the common value is in $(0,\infty)$. No mass in two ways: **disagreement**, $P<1$ (the $d-1$ codirectionality conditions fail; perpendicular gives $m_1=0$, $m_2=\infty$), or **agreement outside $(0,\infty)$** (anti-aligned: $P=1$ and the value is negative; $\mathbf F=\mathbf 0$: $m_1=0$, $m_2$ undefined; $\mathbf a=\mathbf 0$: $m_2=\infty$, $m_1$ undefined). Every mass at $0/0$: both undefined. In 1D $P\equiv1$ and only the sign and zero conditions remain.
