# T72: Fisher consistency does not force sign-dependent reading statistics to cancel

**From:** L13/L18's independent Gaussian scalar slice, L3/L16 and the channel-swap covariance; T36's residual geometry, T39's population criterion and T46's restricted obstruction. The loss below is a counterexample, not an adopted factor.
**Test:** `tests/test_fisher_factor_counterexample.py`.

A sign-dependent, strictly residual-increasing factor has the truth as its unique population optimum at every nonzero fixed signal size. ## Standard mathematics and scope

This is Fisher consistency for orthogonal-regression M-estimation, with Gaussian
polynomial moment identities providing a counterexample to a proposed necessity.
[Zamar (1989), *Biometrika* 76:149–160](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/76.1.149),
§4.1, Theorem 1/Corollary 1, pp. 155–156 (checked in a
[transcription of the published article](https://paperzz.com/doc/7148004/robust-estimation-in-the-errors-in)),
gives sufficient consistency results for residual M-estimation under spherical
errors, appropriate monotonicity/unimodality and scale assumptions, in an iid
structural model. It does not assert necessity of a residual-only loss for
arbitrary fixed pushes. Its §4.2 bounded-loss contamination robustness is
different from T30's bounded noise-shrink evidence contrast.
Original theorem statements in Zamar (1992), *Annals* 20:1875–1888,
and Cheng–Van Ness (1992), *Annals* 20:385–397, remain **unverified**;
they are not invoked as proofs of a classification. The counterexample below
is our calculation and does not depend on an unverified external theorem.

Use one scalar reading with independent Gaussian errors and declared positive
standard deviations. Write $z=(x,y)=(F/\sigma_F,a/\sigma_a)$,
$s=\sigma_F/\sigma_a$, $\theta=\arctan(m/s)\in(0,\pi/2)$, and

$$u_\theta=(\sin\theta,\cos\theta),\qquad
n_\theta=(\cos\theta,-\sin\theta),\qquad
E=x^2+y^2,\quad h=xy,\quad K_\theta=(n_\theta\cdot z)^2.$$

The Gaussian world is $z\sim N(\mu,I_2)$ with $\mu=r u_{\theta_0}$,
arbitrary signed fixed $r$ and arbitrary positive true mass. This is a permitted
slice, not an extension to correlated channels, vector readings or a claim of
uniform consistency for heterogeneous growing arrays.

## 1. An admissible reading-level counterexample to the proposed necessity

Define the loss and relative factor

$$\boxed{\rho(K;E,h)=(E^2+5E)K+h(K^2-6K),\qquad f_\theta(z)=e^{-\rho(K_\theta;E,h)}.}$$

Only this reading enters. $E,h$ are mass-free frame invariants on the scalar
slice. Physical frame reversal sends $(x,y)\mapsto(-x,-y)$ and preserves both;
it is not an arbitrary rotation mixing the force and acceleration channels.
Changing units transforms the data and declared standard deviations together,
and transforms $m$ and $s$ by the same ratio. Channel swap sends
$(x,y,\theta)\mapsto(y,x,\pi/2-\theta)$, preserving $E,h,K$.
An exact-zero reading has $E=h=K=0$ and contributes factor one (Z).
Thus the counterexample already lies in the proposed $\rho(K;s_i)$ form,
with $s_i=(E,h)$; a maximal-invariant classification is unnecessary to refute
the proposed sign restriction. Geometry alone does not turn every mass-free
statistic into a sign-blind one.

For every realized nonzero reading, $0\le K\le E$ and $|h|\le E/2$, so

$$\rho_K=E^2+5E+h(2K-6)
\ge E^2+5E-E(K+3)\ge2E>0.$$

Hence $\rho(0)=0$, $\rho\ge0$ and the factor strictly decreases with residual.
It has the residual's minimizing candidates, when attained, and is finite and strictly positive
on every finite datum. On the closed angular interval it is continuous and
bounded away from zero, so multiplication by any proper prior gives a proper
law for any finite series. These statements do not certify its calibration.

**The sign dependence does not cancel.** Take $z_+=(2,1)$ and $z_-=(-2,1)$.
They have identical component magnitudes, $E=5$, $|h|=2$, and whole-line
$Q=0$, but $h=\pm2$. Both have attainable positive-angle residuals throughout
$1<K<4$. At equal $K$ their loss difference is $4(K^2-6K)$: it is $-32$
at $K=2$ and $-36$ at $K=3$. Thus no common function of $K$ and statistics
blind to this sign flip, plus a data-only additive constant, represents these
losses. Their relative mass evidence really depends on the sign.

## 2. Exact stationarity and a strict global population optimum

At truth put $T=u_{\theta_0}\cdot z\sim N(r,1)$ and
$W=n_{\theta_0}\cdot z\sim N(0,1)$, independently. Then $K=W^2$,
$\partial_\theta K=-2TW$, and

$$h=\sin\theta_0\cos\theta_0(T^2-W^2)+\cos(2\theta_0)TW.$$

The $E^2+5E$ part of $E[\partial_\theta\rho]$ vanishes by oddness in $W$.
For the remaining part, the first term in $h$ is odd after multiplying by
$TW(2W^2-6)$, and the second has expectation proportional to
$E[T^2]\{2E[W^4]-6E[W^2]\}=(r^2+1)(6-6)=0$.
This proves stationarity at every true angle and size; all differentiations
are justified by Gaussian integrability of the polynomial envelopes.

Stationarity alone would not suffice. For an arbitrary candidate define
$b=n_\theta\cdot\mu$, $c=\mu_x n_{\theta,y}+\mu_y n_{\theta,x}$,
$q=n_{\theta,x}n_{\theta,y}$, and $h_\mu=\mu_x\mu_y\ge0$.
Let $R(\theta)=E_\mu\rho(K_\theta;E,h)$. Gaussian moments give

$$\begin{aligned}
E_\mu[EK_\theta]-E_\mu[EK_{\theta_0}]&=(r^2+6)b^2,\\
E_\mu[E^2K_\theta]-E_\mu[E^2K_{\theta_0}]&=(r^4+16r^2+48)b^2,\\
E_\mu[h(K_\theta^2-6K_\theta)]&=h_\mu(b^4-3)+4cb^3+12qb^2.
\end{aligned}$$

For example, the second coefficient is
$(E[T^6]+E[T^4]-3E[T^2]-15)/r^2=r^4+16r^2+48$ for $r\ne0$,
extended continuously at zero. For the third identity, with $D=n_\theta\cdot z$,
Gaussian integration by parts gives
$E[xy g(D)]=h_\mu E[g(D)]+cE[g'(D)]+qE[g''(D)]$;
apply it to $g(D)=D^4-6D^2$ and $D\sim N(b,1)$.
Subtracting the true-angle value therefore gives exactly

$$R(\theta)-R(\theta_0)
=b^2[r^4+21r^2+78+h_\mu b^2+4bc+12q].$$

Since $|b|\le|r|$, $|c|\le|r|$, $q\ge-1/2$ and $h_\mu\ge0$,

$$R(\theta)-R(\theta_0)\ge b^2(r^4+17r^2+72).$$

For $r\ne0$ this is strictly positive whenever $\theta\ne\theta_0$ in the
positive interval: $b=r\sin(\theta_0-\theta)$ vanishes only at truth.
At $r=0$ the population criterion is flat. Sums of these expected losses
retain the unique true optimum if any reading has nonzero signal, without a
signal population law or cross-reading borrowing. This is Fisher consistency,
not the full L11 growth-rate or quoted-width claim.

## 3. What this does not meet, and the brief's stop

This factor does **not** meet T30. For the fixed incompatible physical pair
$(F,a)=(1,-2)$ with $\sigma_F=\sigma_a=\varepsilon$, write
$K=\varepsilon^{-2}k$, where $\inf_{m>0}k=1$ and $\sup_{m>0}k=5$.
The loss is $\varepsilon^{-6}(25k-2k^2)+37\varepsilon^{-4}k$, increasing
throughout $1\le k\le5$. Its log-evidence span is exactly

$$\sup_\theta\rho-\inf_\theta\rho
=52\varepsilon^{-6}+148\varepsilon^{-4}\longrightarrow\infty.$$

Nor is its near-line coefficient $\rho_K(0)=E^2+5E-6h$ the fixed Gaussian
coefficient $1/2$. It is a counterexample to the proposed **consistency-only**
classification, not a factor satisfying all project premises. It does not
contradict T46: its nonlinear $K^2$ term cancels the population score bias,
an operation outside a fixed $w(Q_+)$ multiplying a quadratic criterion.

B25 explicitly says to stop if a Fisher-consistent factor outside the expected
class exists. That case occurs even with a nonnegative loss strictly increasing
in the residual. Accordingly no necessary-and-sufficient classification,
Student-family selection, tail-index choice or mean-one mixture audit is
asserted here. G19/G22 remain open; T30 might restrict the larger class, but
that subsequent intersection has not been classified. Tests check Gaussian
population identities by deterministic quadrature, the symmetry and
noncancellation claims, and the failed T30 contrast above. No coverage study.

## 4. Local continuation: Fisher consistency and bounded confidence together

The joint requirements still permit noncancelling sign dependence on this scalar
Gaussian slice. Let $P(z,\theta)$ denote §1's polynomial loss, fix $a>0$, set
$v=(1+2a)^{-1}$, and define

$$D_a(z,\theta)=e^{-aE}[(E^2+5vE)K+h(K^2-6vK)]
=e^{-aE}v^3P(z/\sqrt v,\theta),$$
$$\rho_{\rm joint}=A\log(1+K/\nu)+\lambda D_a,\qquad A,\nu>0,\quad\lambda>0.$$

**Global population optimum.** Completing the Gaussian square gives the exact identity
$$E_{N(\mu,I_2)}D_a
=v^4 e^{-av\|\mu\|^2}E_{N(\sqrt v\mu,I_2)}P.$$
The multiplier is positive and candidate-independent; the rescaled mean is on
the same true line. Hence §2's strict global minimum transfers, not just stationarity.
Writing $b=n_\theta\cdot\mu$, its population loss gap is at least
$$v^5e^{-avr^2}b^2(v^2r^4+17vr^2+72).$$
The logarithmic summand has the same unique optimum by T39 §2's noncentral
chi-square ordering. Their sum is therefore Fisher-consistent at every nonzero
fixed signal, and flat in population at zero signal, for every allowed coefficient.

**Reading properties.** Since $0\le K\le E$ and $|h|\le E/2$,
$$\partial_K D_a\ge e^{-aE}[E(E-K)+2vE]>0\quad(E>0).$$
Both losses vanish at $K=0$ and are nonnegative. Units, physical frame reversal,
swap and Z survive. At equal $K$ the readings $(2,1)$ and $(-2,1)$ differ by
$4\lambda e^{-5a}(K^2-6vK)$, nonconstant on their overlapping feasible interval:
the sign-dependent contribution to relative evidence still cannot cancel.

**T30.** On any fixed scalar incompatible reading scaled by $1/\varepsilon$,
$E=E_0/\varepsilon^2$, $K=k_\theta/\varepsilon^2$, and $0<k_{\min}<k_{\max}$.
The bound $|D_a|\le e^{-aE}(3E^3/2+8vE^2)$ tends to zero uniformly in angle.
The joint log-evidence span consequently tends to $A\log(k_{\max}/k_{\min})$,
finite and strictly positive. For $(1,-2)$ its exact span is
$$A\log\frac{\nu+5/\varepsilon^2}{\nu+1/\varepsilon^2}
+\lambda e^{-5a/\varepsilon^2}(52\varepsilon^{-6}+148v\varepsilon^{-4}),$$
which tends to $A\log5$; unlike §3, the extra term contributes no divergent tail.

**Limit of this finding.** The near-line coefficient is
$A/\nu+\lambda e^{-aE}(E^2+5vE-6vh)$, not the fixed Gaussian $1/2$, even if
$A/\nu=1/2$. Mean-one cut-mixture representability has not been established.
This refutes the proposed sign restriction under the two joint requirements;
it does not settle their intersection with the remaining premises, the full
input type, L11's growing-series rate, or coverage. The same test file checks
the Gaussian change of measure, population gaps, stationarity, invariances,
noncancellation and the finite nonzero tail limit. No factor is adopted.
