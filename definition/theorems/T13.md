## Statement

proper $\pi$ $\Rightarrow$ $\widehat m\in(0,\infty)$; the totality checks pass under ours28 and ours29 (perpendicular, anti-parallel, force only, acceleration only; the all-zero experiment gives median $c$)

## Proof or support

Fix finitely many finite readings with the declared positive definite
$\Sigma_i$ (L2), a full-column-rank declared $N_i$, and $c>0$. Put
$z_i=\Sigma_i^{-1/2}y_i$. In the whitened declared stacked space $W_i$
of T36, a candidate mass defines the subspace
$L_i(m)=\operatorname{col}(\Sigma_i^{-1/2}C_i(m))\subset W_i$.
Since $C_i(m)$ has rank $k_i$, its Gram matrix is positive definite and
$$
 H_i(m)=\lVert P_{L_i(m)}z_i\rVert^2,\qquad
 0\le H_i(m)\le\lVert P_{W_i}z_i\rVert^2=E_i<\infty.
$$
This is the orthogonal-projection bound, including a proper declared
subspace; $E_i$ need not be the full ambient energy.

Take the law in T8 / `../equation.md` as given, with the current
$0<g_i\le1$ and $0\le\eta\le1$, including the prescribed degenerate-case
value of $\eta$. Its data multiplier obeys
$$
 L(m)=\exp\!\left(\frac\eta2\sum_i g_iH_i(m)\right),\qquad
 1\le L(m)\le e^B,\qquad B=\frac12\sum_iE_i<\infty.
$$
For any proper starting probability law $\Pi$ on $(0,\infty)$, therefore,
$$
 1\le Z=\int L(m)\,\Pi(dm)\le e^B<\infty,
 \qquad P(dm)=Z^{-1}L(m)\,\Pi(dm)
$$
is a probability law on the same domain. This also covers general proper
laws without densities; it does not require moments of mass or any
per-reading marginal to be a probability law in $m$ on its own.

Every probability law supported on $(0,\infty)$ has a finite positive
median. Its CDF tends to 0 as $m\downarrow0$ and to 1 as $m\uparrow\infty$.
Choose $0<a<b<\infty$ with $F_P(a)<1/2<F_P(b)$; every median lies between
these bounds. For the current half-Cauchy starting density, $L>0$ makes
the density positive throughout $(0,\infty)$, so the CDF is strictly
increasing and the median is unique. For a general proper law there can
be a median interval; its finite positive endpoints also give a finite
positive logarithmic midpoint under the stated readout convention.

On all-zero data, every projection and $H_i$ is zero, hence $L\equiv1$,
$Z=1$ and $P=\Pi$, for any reading count or declared covariance. The
current half-Cauchy has CDF $(2/\pi)\arctan(m/c)$, so its median is $c$.
For a general proper $\Pi$, properness alone gives a finite positive
median; identifying it with a declared centre also requires that centre
to be a median of the declared law. The empty series has the same result.

This proves totality for the median now in use. The choice of the median
as the unique point functional remains conditional on L23 as in T35;
the argument does not use the older ratio-of-means readout T10 or settle
that separate premise question.
