# T60: A joint trace anchor gives Gaussian calibration on arbitrary identifying histories

**From:** T54's physical common-acceleration graph and exact conditional score, T55's sensitivity/variability decomposition, and T59's separation of local width from global ordering. The common-covariance Gaussian experiment and the trace-anchor family below are explicit conditions.
**Test:** `tests/test_joint_trace_anchor.py`; scalar rate and generator checks in `tests/test_spectral_anchor.py` and `tests/test_weak_anchor_study.py`.
**Status:** proved below, 4 Nov 2026 (Codex). The stated sufficient information condition gives a proper, frame-safe joint score/profile law, global concentration and an observed-standardized Godambe Gaussian limit without a repeated-signal or maximum-leverage assumption. Its physical composites have asymptotically nominal central intervals. Common geometry, Gaussian correctness, the prior regularity and the anchoring class remain conditions; no minimax necessity, heterogeneous-geometry theorem, unique law or production adoption is claimed.

## 1. Physical experiment and invariant finite-data construction

Fix K scalar body masses, spatial dimension d and a positive-definite
covariance Sigma on p=(K+1)d observed coordinates. At event i all bodies
share one arbitrary fixed acceleration a_i in R^d, observed once:

$$C(m)=\begin{pmatrix}m_1I_d\\\vdots\\m_KI_d\\I_d\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Y_i=C(m_0)a_i+\varepsilon_i,\quad
\varepsilon_i\stackrel{\rm ind}{\sim}N(0,\Sigma).$$

The true m0 is fixed and interior positive. No signal distribution or
equality across events is assumed. Common Sigma means common physical
geometry in reference coordinates; independent passive frame changes may
represent it differently in each event. Computation may use each event's
own correctly transformed covariance and whitening.

Let L_N=-sum_i k_i(m)/2 be the Gaussian profile, S_N its mass-vector score,
and C_N the exact conditional score covariance given all own-event
candidate projections. Use the T56 pseudoinverse statistic T_N^2,
including its rank-zero convention. Define observed total whitened energy
and the research anchor, with fixed kappa>0, by

$$E_N=\sum_iY_i^T\Sigma^{-1}Y_i,\qquad
\lambda_N=\frac{\kappa}{\sqrt{1+E_N}},\qquad
Q_N(dm)\propto\exp[-T_N(m)^2/2+\lambda_N L_N(m)]\Pi(dm).$$

Pi is a fixed supplied proper joint prior. The finite construction also
uses the corresponding per-event precisions when covariances differ.
Every weight is positive and at most one. In fact T_N^2<=E_N and
-E_N/2<=L_N<=0, so its weight is bounded below by a positive constant
at fixed data. The law is proper, and divergent nonnegative prior moments
persist. All covariance statements below concern Gaussian limits and
local interval widths, not finite posterior moments.

An exact-zero event changes none of E,L,S,C, so the law is unchanged;
empty and entirely zero data return Pi. Each event's whitened norm is
independent of its chosen whitening or frame. The profile and score
quadratic are invariant under the same bookkeeping, whole-event sign
changes and smooth invertible mass coordinates, with Pi pushed forward.
The family needs no count or true acceleration as an additional input.
These properties do not establish its authority under L19 or select kappa.

### Why the joint scale is trace rather than the pooled largest eigenvalue

For d>=2 a pooled whitened scatter spectrum can depend on independent
event frame choices. Already take K=1,d=2,Sigma=I_4, and two observed
events (F,A)=(e1,e1). Their pooled largest eigenvalue is 4. Rotate only
the second event to (e2,e2); its covariance stays I_4, while that largest
eigenvalue becomes 2. Every scalar candidate profile and conditional score
is unchanged: its graph projector commutes with each common spatial
rotation of the two channels. A largest-eigenvalue anchor would change
the law under this allowed bookkeeping. Total energy is 4 in both cases.

T59's scalar d=1 spectrum does not have this defect: the independent
spatial sign convention leaves each two-channel outer product unchanged.
The present trace choice removes the exhibited joint-frame failure. It
does not prove uniqueness among invariant scales of the required order.

## 2. Information condition and a positive joint limit

For a fixed whitening W, put mu_i=WC(m0)a_i and

$$\Delta_N=\sum_i\|\mu_i\|^2,\qquad B_N^{\rm scale}=N+\Delta_N,
\qquad h_N=\frac{\Delta_N}{\sqrt{N+\Delta_N}}\longrightarrow\infty.$$

The a_i can follow any deterministic fixed history satisfying this
condition. Their directions and sizes need not repeat or have vanishing
maximum leverage. This is a sufficient theorem condition, not a new input
or a proved minimax criterion for the joint model.
For the limit claims, assume Pi has a positive continuous Lebesgue density
near m0 and no additional singular prior component in that neighbourhood.

Let mhat_N be the interior global profile maximum, which exists uniquely
with probability tending to one, and define observed matrices

$$\widehat H_N=-\nabla^2L_N(\widehat m_N),\qquad
\widehat C_N=C_N(\widehat m_N),\qquad
M_N=\widehat C_N^{-1/2}\widehat H_N.$$

With probability tending to one Hhat and Chat are positive definite.
On that event their displayed inverses are ordinary positive-definite
inverses; arbitrary reporting conventions on its complement do not affect
the theorem. The law itself always uses its stated pseudoinverse statistic.

**Conclusion.** Q_N concentrates at m0, and

$$\operatorname{TV}\!\left(
Q_N\{M_N(m-\widehat m_N)\in\cdot\mid Y\},N_K(0,I_K)\right)\to0$$

in sampling probability, while

$$M_N(\widehat m_N-m_0)\Rightarrow N_K(0,I_K).$$

Thus the data-estimated local width matrix is
Hhat_N^-1 Chat_N Hhat_N^-1, with every eigenvalue of order h_N^-2.
The actual pushforward law of any fixed smooth scalar composite with
nonzero gradient at truth has asymptotically nominal equal-tailed central
intervals. Sum, parallel sum and reciprocal coordinates are included.
No finite-noise exact-coverage or finite-moment convergence claim follows.

## 3. Geometry gives uniform signal and noise information bounds

All matrices in this paragraph are evaluated at truth. Let P project
onto X=WC(m0), R=I-P, and D_j=[0;...;I_d;...;0] be the jth mass
design derivative. Write P_j for the corresponding projector derivative.
Define deterministic sensitivity and graph metric by

$$t^TA_Nt=\sum_i\|RW(\textstyle\sum_j t_jD_j)a_i\|^2,\qquad
G_{jk}=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(P_jP_k),\qquad
B_N=A_N+NG.$$

Here B_N is score variability; B_N^scale above is only its scalar order.
There are geometry-dependent constants 0<c<C<infinity, independent of
the signal allocation, such that

$$c\Delta_N I_K\preceq A_N\preceq C\Delta_N I_K,\qquad
G\succ0,\qquad
c(N+\Delta_N)I_K\preceq B_N\preceq C(N+\Delta_N)I_K.$$

For the first lower bound, RW D(t)a=0 means D(t)a belongs to col(C).
Its zero acceleration block forces the plane coefficient to zero;
for nonzero a,t this is impossible. Minimizing the resulting continuous
squared norm over unit a and t gives a strictly positive constant.
Also ||WC a||^2 is uniformly comparable to ||a||^2. The same argument
applied to RW D(t)(C^T Sigma^-1 C)^(-1/2) proves G>0. Upper bounds follow
by continuity. Thus no spanning condition on the acceleration directions
is required for these scalar body masses.

For analysis choose common reference whitening and write F_N=sum_i z_i z_i^T.
Its mean is NI_p+sum_i mu_i mu_i^T, and Gaussian moments give

$$E\|F_N-EF_N\|_F^2=(p+1)(pN+2\Delta_N)=O(N+\Delta_N).$$

Every profile derivative and conditional-C entry is a smooth linear
functional of this scatter locally in m. Consequently, at truth,

$$C_N(m_0)-B_N=O_p(\sqrt{N+\Delta_N}),\qquad
-\nabla^2L_N(m_0)-A_N=O_p(\sqrt{N+\Delta_N}).$$

Matrix orders use any fixed finite-dimensional operator norm. They require
no fourth-power bound on the true signal allocation. This analytical
pooled scatter is not used as a frame-dependent anchor state.

## 4. Consistency, exact score normalization and the fitted matrices

Compactify the graph using u=(m,1)/sqrt(1+||m||^2), as in T54.
Distinct channel directions have mean planes with zero intersection.
Outside any fixed neighbourhood of m0, compactness over both candidate
u and unit acceleration directions therefore gives a population profile
gap at least c_delta*Delta_N, uniformly over the signal history.
The uniform empirical error is O_p(sqrt(N+Delta_N))=o_p(Delta_N).
This proves consistency. Locally the population cost Hessian is uniformly
positive of order Delta_N; scatter concentration gives the same property
for the observed cost. Hence the global maximum is uniquely interior
with probability tending to one and mhat_N-m0=O_p(h_N^-1).

Smoothness then gives

$$\widehat H_N=A_N+O_p(\sqrt{N+\Delta_N}),\qquad
\widehat C_N=B_N+o_p(N+\Delta_N).$$

The second statement uses C_N(mhat)-C_N(m0)=O_p((N+Delta_N)/h_N).
Unlike fitted score outer products, this exact conditional-C matrix is
quadratic in the observations, which avoids a maximum-leverage condition.

At truth T54 gives the exact conditional identity
C_N(m0)^(-1/2) S_N(m0) distributed as N_K(0,I_K).
The score equation and local Hessian bounds give
Hhat_N(mhat_N-m0)=S_N(m0)+o_p(sqrt(N+Delta_N)).
Relative consistency of the two C matrices then proves the stated
observed-standardized sampling limit. No triangular-array score CLT or
assumed signal population is needed.

## 5. Trace scale, normalized law and composite quantiles

Total energy has the noncentral chi-square law with pN degrees of freedom
and noncentrality Delta_N. Thus its mean is pN+Delta_N and its variance
is 2pN+4Delta_N. In particular E_N is comparable in probability to
N+Delta_N, with fixed positive comparison constants. Therefore

$$\lambda_N\Delta_N\asymp_p h_N\to\infty,\qquad
\lambda_N\Delta_N/h_N^2=O_p(h_N^{-1})\to0.$$

For bounded t set m=mhat_N+M_N^-1 t. Local Taylor expansion gives
S_N(m)=-Hhat_N(m-mhat_N)+o_p(sqrt(N+Delta_N)) and relative convergence
C_N(m) to Chat_N, uniformly on these neighbourhoods. Hence T_N(m)^2
=||t||^2+o_p(1). The additional profile log variation is
O_p(lambda_N*Delta_N/h_N^2)=o_p(1).

For tightness, throughout a small convex neighbourhood U of truth the
observed negative Hessian is at least c Delta_N I and C_N(m) is at most
C(N+Delta_N)I. Score monotonicity about its zero at mhat gives
T_N(m)^2>=c h_N^2||m-mhat_N||^2. The anchor relative to the global profile
maximum is at most one. The local prior is bounded and positive; a
radius-1/h_N ball supplies normalizing mass of order at least
exp[lambda_N L_N(mhat_N)]*h_N^-K. These facts control the local rescaled
tails by Gaussian tails.

Outside U the uniform profile gap bounds posterior mass by
O_p(h_N^K)*exp[-c lambda_N Delta_N], which tends to zero since
lambda_N Delta_N is comparable to h_N. This needs no log N margin.
Local expansion and both tail bounds prove the unconditioned TV limit.

For composite sampling coverage, the estimated matrices are relatively
consistent for deterministic A_N,B_N. Standardized projection directions
can thus be replaced by deterministic ones. Their possible rotation with
N is harmless: the exact normalized score is spherical Gaussian, and
the fitted-center error differs by o_p(1). The conditional TV limit and
the delta method give the matching composite quantile widths. Neither
the acceleration-direction covariance nor A_N/Delta_N needs a limit.

## Scope and remaining freedom

This supplies a joint, rather than merely scalar, conditional Gaussian
construction on arbitrary histories satisfying the sufficient information
condition. It preserves the shared-observation covariance and uses a
matrix-valued local width, as required by T55's benchmark. The prior's
coupling and physical composite pushforwards remain explicitly supplied.

The asymptotic proof requires one fixed common physical covariance and
graph geometry, up to bookkeeping. The finite formula is invariant for
individually declared covariances more generally, but no arbitrary
heterogeneous-geometry calibration theorem is inferred. Every fixed
kappa>0 succeeds here, as do other invariant scales of the same order;
neither the trace choice nor its finite functional form is forced uniquely.
No noise-scale mixture theorem, full-type minimax necessity, finite-noise
coverage, permitted-state ruling, caller tuning slot or production change
is adopted.

Within the explicitly narrower class of metric-only, event-additive
quadratic states U=sum_i z_i^T M z_i with a common fixed symmetric M,
invariance under every independent whitening gauge O_i forces
O^T M O=M for every orthogonal O, hence M=cI. Thus that class gives trace
energy up to a constant. Additivity, quadratic dependence, a common
coefficient and exclusion of candidate-graph or channel structure are
extra class assumptions; L16 alone does not select them.
