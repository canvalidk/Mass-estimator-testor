## Standard mathematics

Orthogonal regression's scatter matrix and its eigenvector (Anderson 1984); a tempered, or
generalised, posterior whose power is chosen so that its width matches the sampling spread of its
centre (Miller 2021; the Bartlett-type adjustment the pages already use for η, T31); inversion of T45's
score test for the report. The obstruction in (5) is the independence of a Gaussian sample's mean and
differences (T75 (2)). The one in (7) is additivity of log factors (Bissiri, Holmes and Walker 2016).

## The construction

One instrument, scalar readings, independent channels; in noise units $Y_i=(F_i/\sigma_F,a_i/\sigma_a)$ and
a mass is the angle $\theta$, $m=s\tan\theta$. With $B=\sum Y_iY_i^\top$, its eigenvalues $L\ge l$, $H=L-l$, its
principal line $\hat\theta$, $Q$ the least of $v_\theta^\top Bv_\theta$ on the closed arc of positive masses (T73's
leftover), and a smooth count $C=\sum h_b(\|Y_i\|^2)$ that is 0 for a zero reading and has mean at least 1
for every other:

$$d=\min\Big\{1,\ \frac CQ\Big(\frac LQ\Big)^{\alpha}\Big\},\qquad K=d\,\frac{H^2}{L},\qquad
\text{law}\ \propto\ \pi(d\theta)\,e^{-K\sin^2(\theta-\hat\theta)/2},$$

with two additions for a reading on an axis and for tied eigenvalues (`met/scalar_witness.py`). The
point is the median. The report is the hull of the law's central interval and T45's score set. $\alpha>0$
and $b>0$ are free.

In the pages' words: the Gaussian evidence $e^{-H\sin^2/2}$ raised to one power for the whole series, and
that power is the weak-signal share $H/L$ times a discount, "a count of the readings over the leftover,
capped at 1", times a further factor. One weight on the result, none per reading. It is the candidate
of `../../problems/O14.md` (17 Nov evening, 18 Nov) with a zero-safe count.

## Statement

**(1) Exact properties.** The law is proper and continuous in the readings; an exactly zero reading
changes nothing; the all-zero experiment returns $\pi$; the order of readings, the sign of a reading,
units and the channel swap act as they should.

**(2) Readings no mass explains stay spread; readings one mass explains concentrate.** As both σ's shrink
at a fixed ratio on fixed readings, the law tends to a limit with a bounded, non-constant density
against $\pi$ whenever no positive mass fits all of them, and concentrates on the common mass when one
does. Three readings with forces 0.2, 1, 1.4 at acceleration 1: the interval is 0.55 to 1.82 at
σ = 0.1, 0.01 and 0.001. On the same readings a weight per reading collapses onto the middle one
(0.80 to 1.35, then 0.97 to 1.03), because that reading sits exactly on the best line and keeps full
weight.

**(3) On every identifying series the point is consistent and the law's interval covers at its level in
the limit.** For any signals with $\Delta^2/(N+\Delta)\to\infty$, at a fixed mass and a fixed noise ratio:
$d\to1$, $K$ over the information tends to 1, and the central interval's coverage tends to its level.
Simulated (300 series, level 0.95): 0.967 for 200 readings at signal 2; 0.927 for 2000 at 0.5; 0.947 for
one reading at 30 among 100 noise-only; 0.937 for two at 50. Below the theorem's range it does not fall
apart: 0.950 for 20 at signal 1, 0.957 for 20 at 0.5, 0.940 for 20 noise-only, at this one mass.

**(4) The report covers at least at its level on every finite experiment,** because it contains T45's
set (0.95 to 0.997 on the same series). On noise it is every positive mass with probability near its
level (E12: between $p^2$ and $p$; not checked).

**(5) The four do not pin the function.** Every $\alpha>0$, $b>0$ gives (1)–(4). Members differ: on Can's
far pair (5 and 6, each ± 0.1, acceleration 1 ± 0.002, centre 5) the law's interval is 5.10 to 5.88 at
$\alpha=1/8$ and 4.97 to 6.01 at $\alpha=1/16$; the near pair is 5.41 to 5.69 under both. On the
three-reading shape the limiting precisions differ by the factor $14^{\alpha}$, so the limits differ for
every pair of exponents: an uncountable family. The point is not pinned either (E12 gives a second point
with the same symmetries; not checked).

**(6) "Exactly the stated level on identifying series" and "wider when the readings disagree" cannot
both be asked.** Two readings at strong signal: the centre's error and the gap are independent standard
normals in the limit, so an interval whose half-width is multiplied by any $g(\text{gap})\ge1$ covers
$E[2\Phi(z\,g)-1]>p$. Widening by half when the gap exceeds two noise units gives 0.9521 at 0.95. The
witness keeps the first and gives up the second: its discount tends to 1 on every identifying series.

**(7) Final factors that stay bounded on conflicts cannot multiply.** If the final log factors of disjoint
lists added, then for a reading $x$ and a reading $y$ that conflicts with it,
$\operatorname{osc}\ell_x\le\operatorname{osc}\ell_{x,x,y}+\operatorname{osc}\ell_{x,y}$; with (2)'s bound on the two
conflicting lists, the one good reading could never concentrate. So a discount is recomputed for the
joined series from carried state (T12's raw factors multiply; final ones do not).

**(8) Finding: the discount is not a function of the gap in σ's.** On the far pair, making the
acceleration alone more precise takes the discount from 0.128 ($\sigma_a=0.002$) through 0.225, 0.400 and
0.711 to 1 ($\sigma_a=2\times10^{-7}$): $L/Q$ grows and the factor $(L/Q)^\alpha$ removes it. Two direct
readings of a mass, 5 ± 0.1 and 6 ± 0.1, are the limit of an exactly known acceleration, and there this
family discounts nothing. E12 notes the limit; by (6) a law that keeps (3), with a centre and an
interval of the kind (6) covers, cannot keep its discount at strong signal.

**(9) Finding: the addition for a reading on an axis misfires at a large noise ratio.** Its cap is
$K'=\min\{K,1+\min(B_{11},B_{22})^2\}$. With the acceleration known to $2\times10^{-6}$, two agreeing
readings (5.5 and 5.6, each ± 0.1) lie close to an axis in noise units, the cap engages, and the law's
interval is 0.2 to 15.6 where the Gaussian's is 5.41 to 5.69. (3) is a limit at a fixed noise ratio and
says nothing against this; E12's own code returns the same log factor. The construction without the two
additions does not have this defect and does not have (2) for a reading on an axis.

## What normalising erases (with T73 (5) and T77 (4))

Two series have the same normalised Gaussian evidence at every noise scale exactly when their scatter
matrices differ by a multiple of the identity. Two readings that agree on mass 1, $(2,2),(2,2)$, and two
that read 4 and ¼, $(4,1),(1,4)$, differ by $9\,\mathrm{Id}$: identical normalised evidence, and curves
that differ by the constant 9. So no rule that sees only the normalised pooled evidence can concentrate
on the first and stay spread on the second. The missing number is the trace, equivalently the least
value of the curve: E12's "keep the relative curve and its minimum residual" is T73's unnormalised curve.

## Proof

(1), (2), (4), (5): direct from the formulas (E12's case analysis for (2), §2 of its proof, read and
reproduced on the cases tested). (3): `e12-pooled-witness.md`, "Full-rate point consistency and interval
coverage": $E\,h_b\ge1$ gives $C\ge N-O_p(\sqrt N)$ against $Q\le N+O_p(\sqrt N)$, so $d\to1$; T42's scatter
limits give the centre's law and $K/I\to1$; a local normal limit of the law gives the quantiles. Read;
its inputs are T42 and T43, on main since 7 Nov and not checked by Claude then. (6): Kac–Bernstein.
(7): $\ell_{x,x,y}=\ell_x+\ell_{x,y}+c$.

## Against the rows

- **Existence [the working assumption; `docs/existence_argument_audit_2026-10-29.md`, X3].** The first
  model of a part of the premise list: zero neutrality, L11's rate with a calibrated width, bounded
  confidence, a true statement on noise, on one scalar instrument at a fixed noise ratio. Evidence for
  the conjecture on that part, and none for the full type.
- **Uniqueness [F2].** The same four admit a continuum. The step O14's head calls "the step nothing
  derives" (why the amount is expected over observed, capped, to the first power) is here a proved gap:
  the exponent $\alpha$ is free under these four. What would pin it is a further premise.
- **"Readings are evidence, just less" [L21; R-2026-11-17-3], against a calibrated width.** By (6) the
  two conflict at strong signal, and by (8) this family resolves it against the ruling in the limit of an
  exact acceleration. Property N asks for a true statement and not for the stated level, so the premise
  list does not ask for (3)'s "tends to its level"; E12 added it. If "at least its level" is what is
  asked, a persistent widening is allowed (T75 (6) is one) and this family is not forced to give it up.
  Which of the two the goal means is a question (`../bounds.md`).
- **No borrowing [L19].** The discount and the centre are functions of the pooled scatter. Whether that
  is a use of one reading's signal on another is E12's stated open point.
- **Combination [T12].** (7): what is carried from a block is state from which a discount is recomputed.
  The same conclusion as T77 (4), from the discount's side.
- **The E13 direction [`../../problems/O20.md`].** The witness is a law and a point with a starting law:
  on the old line the four requirements are satisfiable, so what E13 gives up is not forced by them.
  And its "what normalising erases" is the reason E13 gives for its curve.
