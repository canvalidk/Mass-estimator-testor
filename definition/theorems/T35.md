# T35: The parametrisation-free point is the median

**From:** L23 (proposed 28 Oct), T5 (the swap)  
**Test:** tests/test_point_composition.py::test_t35_quantiles_commute_with_increasing_maps_of_log_mass; ::test_t35_geometric_mean_is_not_parametrisation_free  
**Status:** **new, 28 Oct (Claude); conditional on L23, which is proposed, not ruled.**  
**Source:** O2, 28 Oct

## Statement

Let $R$ map laws of mass (continuous CDF) to a point. If $R(g_\#p)=g(R(p))$ for every
increasing bijection $g$ of $(0,\infty)$ (L23), then $R(p)=F_p^{-1}(q)$ for one fixed $q\in(0,1)$:
$R$ is a quantile. If also $R$ commutes with the swap $m\mapsto c^2/m$ (T5), which is
decreasing and sends the $q$-quantile to the $(1-q)$-quantile, then $q=\tfrac12$: $R$ is the
median.

## Proof

$F_p$ is an increasing bijection onto $(0,1)$; push every law to the uniform law $U$ on
$(0,1)$ by $g=F_p$ (composed with any fixed increasing bijection $(0,1)\to(0,\infty)$). Then
$R(p)=F_p^{-1}(R(U))$, so $q:=R(U)$ does not depend on $p$. For the swap, take a law
symmetric under it (the half-Cauchy at $c$, say): its $q$- and $(1-q)$-quantiles are $c^2/x$
and $x$, and equivariance makes them equal, so $q=\tfrac12$.

## What it does not show

- **That L23 is a premise.** It is proposed (O2, 28 Oct). T31 already uses the same
  requirement as its reason for evaluating $\eta$ at $\hat\phi$ ("parametrisation-free only
  where the score vanishes"); here it is applied to the point.
- **That the point should be a functional of the law.** That comes from L19 (O2, 28 Oct).
- Laws with flat stretches in the CDF: the median is then an interval; `met.full` returns its
  midpoint in $\ln m$, the swap-covariant choice (27 Oct).
- Weaker requirements leave a family: units and the swap alone admit every location
  functional of $\ln m$ that commutes with negation (the geometric mean $e^{E\ln m}$ among
  them). L23 is what removes it.
