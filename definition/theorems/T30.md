## Scope (Claude's sort, 27 Oct, not ruled)

The requirement holds where the misfit is codirectional (the 30° pair freezes at 9.0825
nats) and fails where it is a sign: an anti-parallel pair has $Q=0$, $g=1$, and its evidence
span grows as SD$^{-2}$. The requirement is kept; the premise under $Q$ is what is sorted as
mis-stated (L8's count, `../../problems/O13.md`, 27 Oct). Candidate repair there:
$g=\min(1,\kappa_k/Q_+)$ with $\kappa_k=\sup_r E[Q_+]$, which is $k-1$ for $k\ge2$ (so this
theorem's acute cases are unchanged) and $(1-2/\pi)/2$ for $k=1$.

**27 Oct pressure result (Codex):** the constants in that proposal are not the supremum
over all permitted positive scalar-mass experiments. O13's next section derives a finite
counterexample at true whitened channel strengths 32 and 1/2, for each k=1,2,3, and proves

`tests/test_positivity_candidate.py` checks the counterexample and the candidate's
