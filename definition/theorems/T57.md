# T57: A vanishing profile anchor can identify a score law without changing its local width

**From:** T54/T55's specified common-acceleration Gaussian protocol, T56's proper but globally nonidentifying score law, and the explicit additional anchoring family below.
**Test:** `tests/test_score_law_obstruction.py`.
**Status:** proved below, 4 Nov 2026 (Codex). In T55's regular repeated-block experiment, a coordinate-invariant, zero-event-safe profile anchor gives a proper globally concentrating score-based law with the Godambe Gaussian local shape. This is a conditional existence/comparison construction. It does not force an anchor, its family, exponent, permitted state or adoption; no prior, physical noise interpretation, production type or ruling is changed.

## 1. Specify the extra global rule

Let D be finitely many independent physical events in T54's declared
joint Gaussian experiment. Write the raw Gaussian profile criterion as

$$L_D(m)=-\tfrac12\sum_{i\in D}k_i(m)\le0,$$

with the declared covariance included in each residual cost. Let S_D be
its mass-vector score and C_D the exact conditional score covariance
given the own-event candidate projections, as in T56. Set

$$T_D(m)^2=S_D(m)^TC_D(m)^+S_D(m).$$

Use T56's explicit convention T^2=0 at observed rank zero. Its stacked
score representation gives S_D in range(C_D), so this is finite and
nonnegative at every finite candidate and finite datum.

Define the profile oscillation over the whole positive mass domain,

$$R_D=\sup_{m>0}L_D(m)-\inf_{m>0}L_D(m),\qquad
\lambda_D=(1+R_D)^{-\alpha},\qquad 0<\alpha<1.$$

The inequalities m>0 are componentwise. Alpha is a fixed parameter of the
research family, not a new physical caller input. For a supplied proper
joint mass prior Pi, the proposed anchored law is

$$Q_D^\alpha(dm)=
\frac{\exp[-T_D(m)^2/2+\lambda_D L_D(m)]\Pi(dm)}
{\int\exp[-T_D(u)^2/2+\lambda_D L_D(u)]\Pi(du)}.$$

The score term supplies the local uncertainty geometry. The vanishing
profile term distinguishes profile maxima from the false profile minima
that T56 shows can have the same score statistic. This rule is additional
to conditional-test inversion and does not follow from it.

Exponentiating a statistical contrast is standard quasi-posterior
mathematics; see Chernozhukov and Hong (2003),
[sections 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.07782v1).
Here the global separation and local tail arguments are established for
the stated construction rather than inferred from exact candidate tests.
The existence result does not give the anchoring family a new authority.

## 2. Finite-data properness and invariances

For every event, 0<=k_i(m)<=E_i, where E_i is its finite whitened observed
energy. Thus L_D is bounded, R_D is finite and nonnegative, and
0<lambda_D<=1. The displayed weight is strictly positive and at most one.
Consequently its normalizer lies in (0,1], for **any** proper supplied
prior, including an atomic one. Smooth projection geometry makes L_D
continuous; its supremum and infimum can be taken over a countable dense
candidate set, ensuring measurability. The pseudoinverse statistic is
measurable as well.

There is also a uniform finite-data lower bound. T50's stacked-map
identity writes T_D^2 as the squared projection of the stacked residual
coordinates onto the score-map column space. Hence

$$0\le T_D(m)^2\le\sum_i\|R_i(m)z_i\|^2
\le E_D:=\sum_i\|z_i\|^2.$$

Since L_D>=-E_D/2 and lambda_D<=1, the anchored kernel lies in
[exp(-E_D),1]. Its normalized density relative to Pi is bounded above
and below by positive finite constants. Thus an infinite nonnegative
prior moment remains infinite at every finite data set. The local
Gaussian width theorem below must not be read as a moment claim.

An appended independent exact-zero physical event adds zero to L_D, S_D
and C_D. It changes neither R_D nor lambda_D, so the anchored law is
exactly unchanged. Empty or entirely zero observed data have L=S=C=0
and return Pi. This is whole-event zero neutrality, not a claim that
zero-valued coordinates inside a shared-scale acquisition carry no
information. A copied observation remains one physical event with its
declared dependence; it is not another independent score contribution.

Under any smooth invertible mass-coordinate change, the profile remains
a scalar function and its range is unchanged. The score and conditional
covariance transform with the same Jacobian. Their stacked-map
representation preserves T^2 even at singular covariance, without
asserting a false general congruence formula for the Moore--Penrose
inverse itself. Therefore the anchored law commutes with that coordinate
change when Pi is pushed forward. Units, frame/whitening bookkeeping
and independent whole-event sign reversal are inherited in the same way.
An added data-only constant in L_D changes neither its oscillation nor
the resulting normalized law.

These facts do not turn the construction into a normalized data
likelihood or select the reference latent measure. There is no
conditional-score determinant multiplier.

## 3. A globally concentrating Godambe limit in the repeated-block regime

Use exactly T55's model: a fixed finite block of L common-acceleration
event types, K scalar body masses, fixed positive-definite covariance
shapes, at least one nonzero fixed true acceleration, and n independent
Gaussian repetitions at fixed noise. Each event is profiled over its
own free acceleration; no equality of nuisance values is supplied to
the fitting procedure. Let m0 be interior. For the TV claim, suppose
Pi restricted to a neighbourhood of m0 has a positive continuous
Lebesgue density, with no additional singular prior component there.
Pi may have heavy tails or singular components elsewhere.

Let mhat_n be the consistent interior profile maximum. Write A>0 for
T55's block sensitivity, B=A+G>0 for its score covariance, and

$$\mathcal I_G=AB^{-1}A,\qquad V=\mathcal I_G^{-1}=A^{-1}BA^{-1}.$$

Then for every fixed alpha in (0,1),

$$Q_{D_n}^{\alpha}\{\,\|m-m_0\|>\delta\,\}\to0
\quad\text{in probability for every }\delta>0,$$

and the anchored law of sqrt(n)(m-mhat_n) converges in total variation,
in sampling probability, to N_K(0,V). Its local center retains

$$\sqrt n(\widehat m_n-m_0)\Rightarrow N_K(0,V).$$

Thus smooth composites with nonzero gradient have asymptotically nominal
central intervals from their actual pushforward laws. This includes sum
and parallel sum of the positive body masses. As in T54/T55, these are
local shape and quantile claims, not convergence of finite-law moments.
The finite-data construction is proper for all priors; the local density
condition is needed for the Gaussian TV theorem. In particular an atom
at truth would not produce that Gaussian TV limit.

### The profile supplies a global gap and the required anchor scale

T55's compactified graph argument gives a continuous expected block
profile M(m), uniquely maximized at m0, and

$$\sup_m|n^{-1}L_{D_n}(m)-M(m)|\to0
\quad\text{in probability}.$$

The supremum can equivalently be taken over the compact closure of
(m_1,...,m_K,1)/sqrt(1+||m||^2). Candidate planes retain full latent rank
there. A nonzero true acceleration rules out containing the true mean
in any different or boundary plane.

Since M is nonconstant, c=osc(M)>0, and therefore

$$R_{D_n}/n\to c,\qquad
\lambda_{D_n}\sim(cn)^{-\alpha},\qquad
\lambda_{D_n}\to0,\qquad
\frac{n\lambda_{D_n}}{\log n}\to\infty.$$

The scale is computed from the mass profile. The procedure does not
receive n, a latent energy, the true accelerations or a support threshold
as an additional input.

### Local expansion and root-n tail control

T56 gives C_n(mhat_n)/n->B and, uniformly on bounded h,

$$S_n(\widehat m_n+h/\sqrt n)=-\sqrt n\,Ah+o_p(\sqrt n),$$
$$T_n(\widehat m_n+h/\sqrt n)^2
=h^T\mathcal I_Gh+o_p(1).$$

The profile expansion about its maximum is O_p(1) on such neighbourhoods.
Multiplication by lambda_Dn->0 makes its additional log variation o_p(1).
Hence the anchor changes neither the local center nor the Godambe
quadratic shape.

For tightness, fix a small convex neighbourhood U of truth. The local
uniform laws, continuity of the population matrices and A,B>0 give,
with probability tending to one, constants a,b>0 such that throughout U

$$-\nabla^2L_n(m)\succeq naI,\qquad
0\prec C_n(m)\preceq nbI.$$

For m and mhat_n in U, integrate the Hessian along their joining segment.
Since S_n(mhat_n)=0, strong monotonicity gives
||S_n(m)||>=na||m-mhat_n||. Thus

$$T_n(m)^2\ge\frac{a^2}{b}\,n\|m-\widehat m_n\|^2.$$

The anchor relative to its value at the profile maximum is at most one:
exp{lambda_Dn[L_n(m)-L_n(mhat_n)]}<=1. The local prior density is bounded
above and below on a smaller neighbourhood. A root-n ball supplies a
normalizer of order at least
exp[lambda_Dn L_n(mhat_n)]*n^(-K/2), in probability. The preceding
quadratic bound then controls the local root-n tails by integrable
Gaussian tails.

### Outside the true neighbourhood no score-root selection is needed

The global profile gap gives, outside any fixed U as above,

$$L_n(m)\le L_n(\widehat m_n)-c_U n$$

with probability tending to one, for some c_U>0. The score kernel is
bounded by one, irrespective of spurious roots or degeneracies. Its
prior-weighted outside integral is therefore at most

$$\exp[\lambda_{D_n}L_n(\widehat m_n)]
\exp[-c_U n\lambda_{D_n}].$$

Dividing by the local normalizer bounds the outside posterior mass by
O_p(n^(K/2))*exp[-c_U n lambda_Dn], which tends to zero. Combining this
global bound with the local Gaussian tail bound and expansion proves
the unconditioned normalized TV limit. No basin is selected by a gate
or by changing prior support.

The logarithmic margin is useful: lambda_n->0 and n*lambda_n->infinity
alone do not generally dominate polynomial neighbourhood-volume
advantages of degenerate wrong roots. The oscillation family above
automatically satisfies the stronger sufficient margin.

## 4. What the construction does and does not force

For T56's explicit two-root example, every alpha in (0,1) suppresses the
false peak while retaining the true peak's Godambe width. At the two
profile eigenlines the profile height difference is asymptotically
-(14/19)n. Their false/true weight ratio consequently acquires the
factor exp[-(14/19)n lambda_Dn+o_p(n lambda_Dn)], which tends to zero.
This repairs that named comparison without changing its supplied
half-Cauchy prior or declared Gaussian noise.

The construction demonstrates compatibility of properness, the stated
symmetries, exact-zero event neutrality, global concentration and
regular local Gaussian calibration in this named regime. It does not
establish full-type calibration, a sharp growth theorem for arbitrary
nuisance histories, finite-noise nominal coverage, or a uniquely selected
global law. Its joint mass law has ordinary composite pushforwards;
agreement with a separately measured composite still requires that
measurement's own declared protocol and justified regime.

Different fixed alpha give different finite-data laws while preserving
the proved properties. Their common limiting success therefore cannot
select alpha or the anchoring class by a score comparison. Alpha is not
licensed as a physical input by writing it into a research preset.
Conditional-test exactness does not force the exponential score kernel,
the raw-profile anchor or this oscillation rule.

The profile oscillation summarizes common-mass evidence; it estimates no
population of signal sizes and predicts no other event's acceleration.
This explains the absence of that particular borrowing operation. It does
not independently settle the admissibility of this experiment state or
global correction under L19. G19/G24/G26/G27 retain their stated measure,
class, uncertainty and protocol obligations. No new premise, prior,
noise model, production type, default or ruling is adopted.

The named test and scalar reference compare population and Gaussian-world
