Setting: one isotropic instrument in noise units, $f=F/\sigma_F$, $\alpha=a/\sigma_a$, $c=s$, so the mass
hypothesis $m$ is the line at angle $\phi$ with $m=s\tan\phi$ (T36 (2)). The reading's 2×2 Gram is
$G_i=\begin{pmatrix}|f|^2&f\cdot\alpha\\ f\cdot\alpha&|\alpha|^2\end{pmatrix}$.

## Statement

**(1) A reading is a pure qubit and the explained energy is the Born rule.** Let $\theta$ be the angle
between $f$ and $\alpha$ and $\psi=(|f|,\ |\alpha|e^{i\theta})\in\mathbb C^2$. Then
$\operatorname{Re}|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|=G_i$, and for the real state $|\phi\rangle=(\sin\phi,\cos\phi)$
$$H_i(\phi)=|\langle\phi|\psi\rangle|^2 .$$
Misalignment is the relative phase. In 2D this is literal ($F$, $a$ as complex numbers); in any $k$ it is
the same statement about $G_i$.

**(2) The whole-line misfit is the reading's imaginarity.**
$Q_i=E_i\,\lambda_{\min}(\operatorname{Re}\rho_i)=\tfrac12\big(E_i-|\textstyle\sum_j\psi_j^2|\big)$,
$\rho_i$ the normalised state. It vanishes iff $\psi$ is real up to a global phase. The geometric imaginarity
of a pure state is $1-\max_{\phi\ \text{real}}|\langle\phi|\psi\rangle|^2$ (Hickey & Gour 2018, the origin of
the resource theory; Wu et al. 2021), and for real $\phi$ the maximum is $\lambda_{\max}(\operatorname{Re}\rho)$
with $\lambda_{\min}+\lambda_{\max}=1$, so $Q_i=E_i\cdot M_g(\psi_i)$ **exactly** (checked 7 Nov against the
definition as quoted in Guo, Li & Fei, arXiv:2410.20879 §III). Lagrange's identity is the same fact:
$\lambda_{\min}\lambda_{\max}=|f|^2|\alpha|^2-(f\cdot\alpha)^2=|f\wedge\alpha|^2$.

**(3) The sign is not a phase.** $\theta=\pi$ (anti-parallel) gives a real state with a negative
amplitude: $Q=0$. ours29's blind spot is that imaginarity cannot see a sign. L1's $m>0$ asks for
*non-negative* amplitudes, a second condition beyond reality; $Q_+$ is the distance to that set. G22's
conflict is phase against sign.

**(4) The default starting law is uniform over real states.** The half-Cauchy at $c=s$ pushed to $\phi$
is $2/\pi$ on $(0,\pi/2)$ (already in O6's table and T28; here it is the rotation-invariant measure on the
real states, restricted to non-negative amplitudes).

**(5) The flat law of a series is an axial von Mises, and it cannot tell misalignment from
disagreement.** With $G=\sum_iG_i$, $E=\operatorname{tr}G$ and $R$ the length of the summed real Bloch
vector, $\sum_iH_i(\phi)=E/2+(R/2)\cos(2\phi-2\hat\phi)$: in $\exp(H/2)$, a von Mises on the doubled
angle with concentration $R/4$ (axial data; Mardia & Jupp 2000, *Directional Statistics*, ch. 9). Every
real PSD 2×2 matrix is $\operatorname{Re}$ of one pure qubit (its Bloch disc is the shadow of the sphere),
so one misaligned reading and several aligned readings that disagree can have the same $G$ and therefore
the same flat law. Within $G$, the confidence lost to misalignment (g's job) and to disagreement (η's
job) are one quantity: the shortening of $R$ below $E$.

**(6) But no tempering that reads only $G$ can do both jobs (Claude, provisional).** Along
$\sigma\to0$ a fixed misaligned pair's Gram is $tG_0$, $t\to\infty$; a long aligned noisy series on one
instrument has $G_N/N\to G_\infty$, so its Grams also run along rays. T30 needs the tempered evidence
bounded on the first, so $\eta(tG_0)=O(1/t)$; η's target needs $\eta\to\Delta/(\Delta+M)$, constant along
the second. Where the rays coincide (by (5) they can, exactly), one function of $G$ cannot do both. What
tells the cases apart is a **count**: how much misfit noise alone makes ($k_i-\tfrac12$ per reading, $M$
per series). Z forbids a series-level count (an exact-zero reading adds $k$ to $M$ and nothing to $G$:
O14, 4 Nov, item 3); a per-reading count is Z-safe because a zero reading's count multiplies its own zero
energy. **So the reading boundary does work the data's $G$ cannot: it puts the null count where the
energy is.** That is a reason for g's per-reading form from T30 and Z, independent of L8's shared state.
The argument assumes T30's bound is locally uniform in the pair's shape; not written out.

**(7) The series half does come out of the picture.** $\eta_G=1-Q/H(\hat\phi)$, with $Q$ the series'
whole-line misfit and $H(\hat\phi)$ its explained energy at the best line (`met.full.gram_eta`), equals
$1-\lambda_{\min}(G)/\lambda_{\max}(G)$ on one instrument. It is the size law's shrinkage
$1-M/H^2$ (O14) with the declared count $M$ replaced by the series' own misfit $Q$, whose expectation
under the model is about $M$. Exact-zero readings leave it unchanged; units, the swap, frame rotations and
the choice of $c$ do not move it. On O14's world it tracks $\Delta/(\Delta+M)$ far more closely than the
Bartlett η (RMS distance 0.003–0.52 against 0.10–0.74; strong signal 0.003–0.05 against 0.10–0.18,
no cap problem); at small $N$ and weak signal it still overshoots (0.66 against 0.19 at $k=1$, $N=5$,
snr 0.5). Seeded table in `../../problems/O14.md`, 7 Nov.

## Phase-two sort (Claude, provisional, 7 Nov)

Where each ingredient of the picture comes from, in GOAL.md's three bins. This is a sort, not a result; B23
checks the second row, E6–E9 (`../../explore/README.md`) the rest.

| ingredient | bin | from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hypotheses are rays (a mass is a line through the origin) | Newton | $F=ma$ linear and homogeneous (L1) |
| hypotheses are *real* states | Newton | scalar inertia and frame independence (L22, L16); in 2D rotations alone leave a phase and the sign convention removes it (B23) |
| amplitudes are non-negative | Newton | $m>0$ (L1) |
| the inner product | measurement | units leave only the noise metric (T36 (1)); isotropy in noise units (L9) |
| evidence is a squared projection | measurement | the Gaussian model (L13), itself only conditionally forced (Herschel–Maxwell, T6's route) |
| one phase gauge per reading | measurement | incidental pushes; no borrowing between readings (L19); frame independence (L16) |
| the $i$ itself | space | two vectors span an oriented plane, and an oriented Euclidean plane is $\mathbb C$ |

What QM has and this lacks is interference: amplitudes adding across readings before the square. Here each
reading's gauge is fixed separately, so the evidence adds as $\operatorname{Re}\rho$, incoherently. The premise
that forbids a shared phase reference is L19 (readings joined only through $m$); whether that is the right
reading of what no-borrowing forbids is exploration E6.

## What it does not show

- Anything with anisotropic or correlated noise (O17), or several instruments: the qubit form needs the
  hypothesis subspace to be one real state times the identity. $\eta_G$ is *defined* for any $\Sigma_i$
  (whole-line misfit over best-line energy) but only tested on one instrument.
- That $\eta_G$ meets property N, L11, or composition: no coverage has been run (brief B22). Nor that a Z-safe
  estimate of $\Delta/(\Delta+M)$ with less small-$N$ bias exists within functions of $G$ (exploration E8).
- That the analogy is more than shared ancestry: the Born *form* (a squared projection as the sufficient
  statistic) appears, not the Born rule; what Newton supplies is the real, projective skeleton (the sort above).
  Real-amplitude quantum theory is falsified (Renou et al. 2021), so this is the toy's boundary, not a bridge.
- That the T48/ours33 line's per-reading noise size changes (6): there the reading boundary also carries
  a shared latent, a second reason for it.
- That the analogy reaches QM's use of phase: here the data are complex and the hypotheses real, and the
  law keeps only $\operatorname{Re}\rho$; phase shows up as lost fidelity, never as interference.
