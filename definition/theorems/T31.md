## Statement

$\eta=\min(1,\mathcal H/J)$ with $\mathcal H=-\sum_i\ell_i''$, $J=\sum_i\ell_i'^2$ at the series' best line $\hat\phi$ on the **whole projective line** of slopes (negative and infinite $m$ included), $\ell_i=g_iH_i/2$; $\eta=1$ when $J=0$. Forced: $\mathcal H/J$ by property N in the long-series limit (the width must track how far the point moves under repeats); one $\eta$ per series because $m$ is one number; **the raw $J$** (no $N/(N-1)$) by Z, since an exact zero reading has $\ell_i'=\ell_i''=0$ and a Bessel factor counts it (ours25's sandwich moved on appended zeros); the whole line because on $m>0$ the best line can sit at a boundary where the curvature measures nothing (ours25 then fell back to the prior); evaluation at $\hat\phi$ because the ratio is parametrisation-free only where the score vanishes (the swap follows); candidates unchanged by L19 (shrinking each cloud by $\eta$ would infer signal size from the series). Natural, not forced: the cap at 1; the power form at finite $N$ (O14). Removes the $\sqrt N$ growth of the null tilt and leaves a bounded floor (O15). Tested 1D–3D: quoted width 93.7–97% wherever there is signal at $N=200$; the point stops wandering on pure noise.
