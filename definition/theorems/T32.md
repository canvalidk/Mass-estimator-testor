## Statement

for $\Sigma_i=\mathrm{diag}(\sigma_F^2,\sigma_a^2)\otimes I_k$ and $N_i=I$: with $M_i$ the reading's whitened Gram matrix (30 Sept dictionary), $Q_i=\lambda_-(M_i)$, and the product of the two pairings is $M_i$'s squared correlation, $P=M_{12}^2/(M_{11}M_{22})$. So $Q_i\,\lambda_+=M_{11}M_{22}(1-P)$ and $Q_i=\tfrac12\big[T_i-\sqrt{T_i^2-4M_{11}M_{22}(1-P)}\big]$: the dimensionless disagreement $1-P$ converted into noise units of energy by the sizes. The physical and whitened $P$ coincide when the channel noise is diagonal; with cross-channel correlation $\rho$ the law sees the whitened one ($P=0.62$ physically, $0.71$ at $\rho=0.5$, $0.97$ at $\rho=-0.7$). $g$ is not a function of $P$ alone and must not be: the same angle at scales $0.1,1,10$ gives $Q=0.002,0.21,21.1$ and $g=1,1,0.09$ (a small misaligned pair is consistent with alignment under noise). At equal whitened energies $Q=s^2(1-\lvert\cos\theta_w\rvert)$ exactly.

## Proof or support

For the unrestricted isotropic case in the statement, put
$x=\widetilde F/\sigma_F$, $y=\widetilde a/\sigma_a$,
$A=|x|^2$, $B=|y|^2$, $C=x\cdot y$, $s=\sigma_F/\sigma_a$ and $t=m/s$.
Direct minimization over the latent acceleration gives the squared correction cost

$$
K(t)=\frac{A+Bt^2-2Ct}{1+t^2},\qquad H(t)=A+B-K(t).
$$

$K$ is the Rayleigh quotient of $\begin{pmatrix}A&C\\C&B\end{pmatrix}$ at
$(1,-t)$. Searching the whole projective line therefore gives
$Q=\lambda_-=(A+B-\Delta)/2$, where $\Delta=\sqrt{(A-B)^2+4C^2}$.
Also $\lambda_-\lambda_+=AB-C^2=AB(1-P)$, proving the stated pairing identity
(when $AB=0$, use $AB-C^2$ directly rather than the undefined correlation).

### Domain of the misfit (Codex derivation, 26 Oct; no changed premise)

The eigenvalue above permits negative mass. On the physical domain $t>0$, the exact bounds are

$$
Q_+:=\inf_{t>0}K(t)=
\begin{cases}\lambda_-&C>0,\\\min(A,B)&C\le0,\end{cases}
\qquad
\sup_{t>0}K(t)=
\begin{cases}\lambda_+&C<0,\\\max(A,B)&C\ge0.\end{cases}
$$

This follows by differentiating: the stationary points satisfy
$Ct^2+(B-A)t-C=0$. For $C>0$ the positive stationary point is a minimum;
for $C<0$ it is a maximum. The other bounds are the endpoints $K(0)=A$ and
$K(\infty)=B$. At $C=0$, $K$ is monotone or constant. Thus

$$
Q_+-Q=\begin{cases}0&C\ge0,\\(\Delta-|A-B|)/2&C<0,\end{cases}
\qquad
\operatorname{osc}_{t>0}K=\frac{\Delta+|A-B|}{2}.
$$

Endpoint bounds can be unattained; none assigns a finite fitted mass to zero or infinity.
The difference $Q_+-Q$ is the additional cost imposed by positive mass. In particular
negative collinearity has $Q=0$ but $Q_+=\min(A,B)>0$ when both channels are nonzero.
This is present already for scalar readings, whose $\nu=k-1$ is zero.

The current $Q$ therefore measures collinearity without its sign; it is not a complete
positive-mass compatibility cost. This is a domain qualification of T32, not a proof
that the law must return a different answer. Replacing $Q$ inside $g$ is not justified
by this identity; the positivity inequality is not an additional fixed equality degree
of freedom. The unresolved role in the mass operation is recorded in O13.
