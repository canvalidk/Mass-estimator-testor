# T25: Zero components carry nothing; $k$ does

**From:** T8

## Statement

measured exact zeros on some axes of a signal reading leave $H_i$ exactly as without those axes, at every $m$, whatever their $\sigma$. The law changes only through $k_i$. A 3D reading declared $k=1$ equals the 1D reading exactly.
