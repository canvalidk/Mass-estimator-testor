# Rulings

Every decision Can has made about the mass estimator, in date order, one entry each. This
file is **append-only**: a later ruling that changes an earlier one gets a new entry that
names what it replaces; the old entry stays. A status anywhere in `definition/` or
`problems/` may say "ruled" only if the date is in here.

Each entry gives his words verbatim where a record quotes them (spelling and typos kept),
`[paraphrase]` where a record only paraphrases, and the record and line where it stands.
Ids are `R-YYYY-MM-DD-n`. Claude's analyses, proposals and offers are not rulings and are
not here; rulings that records say are **owed** are listed at the end.

Built 2026-10-24 by extracting every ruling from the records in `docs/` and the VD-docs
originals; 28 of the verbatim quotes were re-checked against their sources by a second
read. Line numbers refer to the files as they stood that day.
