# O9: One reading or several: the window criterion

**Status:** open. **Kind:** ruling. **Last updated:** 2026-10-24.

## The item (lemma list d4, verbatim)

Repeated measurements of one unchanged push: are they one reading (one scale) or several? L19 treats readings as separate pushes. **10 Oct, proposed:** the criterion is the window (T33). Same window: one reading, one signal, combined $\Sigma$. Different windows: separate readings, L19 as written. That the push is "unchanged" does not enter: it would be a statement about signals across readings.
