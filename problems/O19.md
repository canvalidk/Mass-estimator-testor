# O19: What information growth makes consistency attainable?

**Status:** open; T41/T52 refute universal latent-energy promises. T53 gives an estimator-independent sharp threshold for a specified uniform Gaussian energy class, matching T42's procedure-specific threshold. A full-type replacement remains unsorted.
**Kind:** proof and premise sort. **Last updated:** 2026-11-04 (Codex).
**Links:** `../definition/theorems/T41.md`; `../definition/proof.md` G25;
L11 and L16 in `../definition/premises.md`; T17; O15; O14 G24.

## The item

L11 currently states that the point converges to the true mass whenever total
true push energy diverges. T41 proves that this is impossible over the current
unrestricted reading type, independently of the estimator, its law, calibration,
or readout. The task is to identify and justify the information-growth condition
that a valid consistency statement needs.

**Provenance.** The universal wording is carried from lemma-list d4
(`../docs/vd-docs-context/mass_estimator_lemmas_2026-10-10.md`, L11), graded
strong; it is the current mathematical specification. This finding does not
attribute those full asymptotic quantifiers to a new verbatim ruling by Can.
L16's own-frame and sign-convention requirement is recorded in
R-2026-09-28-3: "Yes of course. Bookkeeping can't matter."
No premise or ruling is changed here.

## What would close it

A justified growth condition, stated with its nuisance and sampling quantifiers,
and a proof of consistency under that condition. It must retain the ordinary
identifying regimes already proved in T17 and T39 without promising recovery
from an experiment carrying insufficient distinguishing information.

The condition's place in the definition must also be sorted: a consequence of
the declared experimental structure, a genuine input that practice supplies, or
a correction to L11's statement. An unobserved excitation threshold or a tuning
constant is not made a caller input merely to evade the counterexample
(`../TASK.md` section 5). Neither a replacement condition nor a new input is
selected by this item.

## Attempts

### 3 Nov: a universal weak-signal obstruction (Codex)

**Standard mathematics:** Le Cam's two-point method with mixtures, also called
fuzzy-hypothesis testing. The method compares induced sampling distributions;
auxiliary distributions over deterministic nuisance parameters can prove a
lower bound without becoming part of the estimator's model. See Polyanskiy
and Wu, [Dualizing Le Cam's method, introduction](https://arxiv.org/html/1902.05616).
The experiment and application below are Codex's; T41 gives the theorem and
its checks.

Use one-dimensional force and acceleration readings, declared covariance
$I_2$, full candidate subspace, and one fixed positive mass. The compared
experiments use identical declared inputs, including the same starting law.
Write

$$u_m=(m,1)/\sqrt{1+m^2},\qquad
r_i=b/\sqrt i,\qquad
Y_i=\epsilon_i r_i u_m+Z_i,\quad Z_i\sim N(0,I_2),$$

independently across readings. Every fixed sign stream
$\epsilon_i\in\{-1,1\}$ is a permitted deterministic experiment. It satisfies
Newton II and persistence exactly. Its total true push energy is
$\sum_{i\le N}r_i^2\to\infty$, while $\sum_i r_i^4=b^4\pi^2/6<\infty$.
The true acceleration energy also diverges, by the fixed positive factor
$1/(1+m^2)$.

For the proof only, average over independent equiprobable signs. Relative to
the zero-mean Gaussian reading law, a single mixed reading has density ratio

$$L_{m,i}(y)=e^{-r_i^2/2}\cosh(r_i u_m^Ty),\qquad
E_0[L_{m,i}^2]=\cosh(r_i^2).$$

Let $Q_{m,N}$ denote the resulting sampling mixture and $P_{0,N}$ the pure
noise law. Independence and $\log\cosh x\le x^2/2$ give

$$1+\chi^2(Q_{m,N}\Vert P_{0,N})
=\prod_{i\le N}\cosh(r_i^2)
\le\exp\left(\tfrac12\sum_{i\le N}r_i^4\right).$$

For two masses put $\rho=u_{m_0}^Tu_{m_1}$,
$S_N=\sum_{i\le N}\log\cosh(r_i^2)$, and
$C_N=\sum_{i\le N}\log\cosh(\rho r_i^2)$. Their cross moment under pure
noise is $E_0[L_{m_0,N}L_{m_1,N}]=e^{C_N}$. Cauchy--Schwarz applied
directly to the difference of their density ratios therefore gives

$$\|Q_{m_0,N}-Q_{m_1,N}\|_{\rm TV}
\le\sqrt{(e^{S_N}-e^{C_N})/2}
\le\sqrt{\operatorname{expm1}\left(\tfrac12\sum_i r_i^4\right)/2}.
$$

Taking $b=1/2$ bounds the right-hand side by
$\sqrt{\operatorname{expm1}(\pi^2/192)/2}=0.1624012272292\ldots$
for every N. Therefore every test distinguishing these masses has
equal-prior error probability at least $0.4187993863854\ldots$.
No such tests can become consistent.

If one estimator were consistent for every fixed infinite sign stream at
both masses, its bounded error probabilities would converge to zero for
each stream. Dominated convergence would make its sign-mixture errors
converge to zero too. Testing by which of the two masses is nearer its point
would contradict the bound. **No estimator satisfies literal L11 on all
these deterministic experiments.** This is a point-identification
obstruction, not merely failure of a law to concentrate or an interval to
cover. Changing the median cannot remove it.

The signs were introduced only to prove this statement about fixed nuisance
streams. They are not a declared signal-size distribution, a learned
population of setups, or an additional input. The proof neither assumes nor
asks the definition to infer such a population.

### L16 strengthens it to the all-positive amplitude schedule

In this identity-covariance, full-subspace experiment, simultaneously reversing
force and acceleration in one reading is a change of that reading's sign
convention. L16-invariance therefore gives, for every finite sign pattern,

$$T_N(\epsilon_1Y_1,\ldots,\epsilon_NY_N)
=T_N(Y_1,\ldots,Y_N).$$

All declared geometric inputs are transformed too; the identity covariance
and full subspace remain the same geometric objects. Gaussian noise is sign
symmetric. Consequently the output law of an invariant estimator in the
all-positive mean experiment equals its output law under every fixed sign
pattern and their proof mixture.

The same testing bound therefore applies directly to the all-positive
$r_i=b/\sqrt i$ schedule: no L16-invariant estimator can be consistent at
both masses there. This does not claim that changing physical push direction
is itself bookkeeping. It uses bookkeeping invariance to identify estimator
distributions in these sampling experiments.

### 3 Nov, continuation: a sharp criterion for the flat Gaussian law (Codex)

T42 (`../definition/theorems/T42.md`) supplies a necessary and sufficient
consistency criterion for a **specified method and experiment**, not a
replacement for L11 over the full type. Use independent common-isotropic 1D
Gaussian errors, a fixed positive interior mass, a fixed half-Cauchy starting
law, the unweighted flat Gaussian factors, and the current whole-line
Bartlett power. Let the true signed amplitudes be arbitrary deterministic
numbers, put $\Delta_N=\sum_{i\le N}r_i^2$, and let $N\to\infty$. Then the
continuous law and its current median are consistent precisely when

$$S_N=\frac{\Delta_N^2}{N+\Delta_N}\longrightarrow\infty.$$

**Positive direction.** This is a signal-to-scatter-fluctuation condition:
the population eigenvalue gap is $\Delta_N$, while the scatter error is
$O_p(\sqrt{N+\Delta_N})$. T42 proves the fitted direction and the quoted
law concentrate under this condition, with arbitrary deterministic amplitudes
and signs. The consistency result requires neither iid signals nor a
maximum-leverage condition. It does not claim a central limit theorem or
nominal interval coverage under every such sequence.

The observable quoted angular precision also diverges:

$$\eta_N\mathcal H_N
=\min(\mathcal H_N,\mathcal H_N^2/J_N)\longrightarrow\infty
\quad\hbox{in probability}.$$

Here $\mathcal H_N$ and $J_N$ are evaluated at the actual whole-line best
fit, and the existing $J=0$ fallback gives $\eta=1$. The ratio
$\mathcal H^2/J$ alone is not the capped precision: one reading has zero
fitted score even on weak data. These finite precision values refer to the
noise-angle coordinate; unlike $\mathcal H/J$, precision is not unchanged
by arbitrary reparametrization.

**Converse.** No bounded-amplitude assumption is needed. If $S_N$ fails to
diverge, take a subsequence on which
$\Delta_N/(2\sqrt N)\to\kappa<\infty$ and
$\sum_i r_i^4/N\to q\in[0,4\kappa^2]$. In the true-line coordinates the
normalized scatter contrast has limit
$Z=(\kappa,0)+G$, $G\sim N(0,I_2)$. The fitted quoted precision has the
finite random limit

$$\frac{4|Z|^2}{1+(q/4)\sin^2(\arg Z)}.$$

An open event has a fitted positive line separated from truth and a finite
limiting law whose median is separated from truth. That event retains
positive probability. Thus the median is not consistent, rather than merely
having an unproved concentration argument. This method-specific converse is
different from T41's estimator-independent impossibility example.

**What the diagnostic is licensed to mean.** R-2026-09-30-5 permits series
width calculations from the readings' own residuals; $\mathcal H$ and $J$
already use that state. Their use in a theorem or an inspection diagnostic
does not infer a common signal-size population, predict another reading's
signal, or require a repeated setup. The estimator need not know the latent
$\Delta_N$: it appears in the performance theorem, not in the input or a
data-dependent acceptance branch. No threshold is used to suppress an output.
Nor is local precision alone a universal certificate: G22's biased criterion
and T39's polynomial tails need their separate arguments.

**The vanishing-power extension of T40.** Write $D_N=\widehat L_N-L_N$ and
let $Q_N\propto\pi\exp(-\eta_ND_N)$ be the reference powered law. For
either T40 operator, the scaled-deviance conditions

$$\eta_ND_N=O_{Q_N}(1),\qquad a_N\eta_N\to\infty$$

implies total-variation convergence of the transformed law to $Q_N$, even
when $\eta_N\to0$. The first condition is posterior tightness of the
**scaled** deviance, in probability over the data. T40's bounds make the
nonlinear remainder tend to zero on bounded scaled deviance; the relative
weight lies in $[0,1]$, giving convergence of normalized laws.

For the bounded monotone rational and exponential weights of T40, the
oscillation construction has $a_N\asymp_p N$ in this Gaussian experiment
even when the amplitudes are not iid. Independence and boundedness control
its fluctuation; the noncentral Gaussian reading energy stochastically
dominates the zero-signal energy, giving a uniform positive mean contribution.
T42 verifies the scaled-deviance and $a_N\eta_N$ conditions under $S_N\to\infty$
and on the bounded-information subsequences. A subsequence argument makes
the transformed and reference laws TV-close for every deterministic Gaussian
signal array as N grows. The same wrong-angle event transfers median failure,
so the research families inherit the sharp criterion too. The operator family, saturation, constants,
and global use of that new state under L19 remain unforced, as G24 records.
No such state or map is adopted here.

**Proposed sort, Codex's.** Newton II fixes what an identifying exact limit
returns. Statistical information growth determines which noisy experiment
sequences approach that limit. The source argument calls the law the
"aligned zero-noise limit"
(`../docs/vd-docs-context/why_the_estimator_is_the_definition.md`, lines 328–330).
Fixed-N noise reduction at fixed noise geometry and nonzero compatible signal lies in an identifying
regime; it does not imply that every divergent-energy history does. T42
supports this separation without selecting a new universal premise, physical
input, or gate. L11's statement and grade are not rewritten.

## What the obstruction names

**Codex's mathematical sort:** the false implication is that divergent total
signal energy is, by itself, sufficient identifying information when the
reading-specific signals are unrestricted. Newton II, positive scalar mass,
persistence, and correct Gaussian noise all hold in the counterexample.
L19's unrestricted setups allow it; no borrowing premise is needed for the
universal impossibility proof. L16 strengthens the conclusion but is not
needed for that proof either.

This names a mis-statement in the universal sufficiency clause of L11; its
replacement remains unsorted. It does not select $\sum r_i^4\to\infty$ as
a universally sufficient replacement, prescribe weights, or supply a new
physical input. The existence working assumption cannot discount the
contradiction. Because L11 is graded strong, any narrowing must be made
explicit, with its justification, rather than hidden in a new candidate.

T39 and T40 remain results in their named regimes. Their fixed-amplitude and
fixed-nonzero-signal arguments are not refuted. What no G24 calibration or
readout can do is restore literal L11 on every allowed divergent-energy
sequence. O15's exact-null and finite-interval issue is related weak
identification, but this example has nonzero signals at every reading and
divergent total energy.

### 4 Nov: unknown allocation and an attainable uniform class (Codex)

**Standard mathematics:** sparse Gaussian mixture testing and minimax
rank-one matrix denoising. T52 uses an auxiliary distribution over allowed
fixed supports; T53 uses a different auxiliary sign mixture on an exact
energy shell. Neither distribution is an estimator input or a physical
population of signals. The distinction between pointwise consistency on
fixed streams and uniform consistency over finite experiment classes is
essential here.

**T52: even divergent oracle information is insufficient.** Fix b=1/2.
In a proof-only infinite allocation measure, activate index i independently
with probability p_i=1/[2(i+1)]. Almost every resulting fixed support S is
infinite. Each active reading has mean b*u_m with positive force and
acceleration, and each inactive reading has mean zero. There are no unknown
signs in this construction. On every such support, all positive-power
energy sums diverge, including sum r_i^4/(1+r_i^2). Nevertheless the induced
sampling mixtures at two distinct masses have TV at most 0.153068 at every
prefix, so their equal-weight binary testing error is at least 0.423466.
Dominated convergence over this one measure on infinite supports rules out
an estimator consistent at both masses on every fixed infinite support.

An oracle given the support could consistently recover the line by using
only the active readings. Thus a criterion based on information available
*after revealing the nuisance allocation* cannot by itself justify a
universal promise with the original information set. This rules out the
tempting replacement of L11 by a quartic or known-magnitude-information sum
alone. It neither proves all sparse histories fail nor selects an allocation
model, support input or rejection threshold.

**T53: the energy threshold is sharp for a specified uniform question.**
In the independent identity-covariance Gaussian experiment, take a fixed
compact interior positive-mass angle interval and all deterministic signed
amplitude vectors of length N with exactly sum r_i^2=Delta_N. Uniform point
consistency over that class is possible if and only if

$$\frac{\Delta_N^2}{N+\Delta_N}\longrightarrow\infty.$$

The ordinary leading scatter line supplies the upper bound without being
given Delta_N. The lower bound applies to every estimator, even one given
Delta_N: all worlds in its auxiliary sign mixture have exactly that energy.
The theorem is a direct small-dimensional rank-one matrix-denoising result;
its literature connection and complete testing proof are in T53.

This advances T42's threshold from a characterization of one method to a
necessary and sufficient threshold for this **uniform energy-class** task.
It is not a necessary condition for learning every individual fixed history,
and does not turn a total-energy condition into a sufficient full-type
statement. The sparse fixed histories in T52 and the class in T53 answer
different quantified questions. Knowing that a particular history has a
favorable oracle information sum does not certify that an unknown-history
procedure can exploit it.

### Proposed scope of the consistency obligation (Codex; not a ruling)

The mathematically defensible target separates two limits:

1. In an identifying fixed finite compatible experiment, hold the declared
   noise geometry and nonzero signal configuration fixed and reduce the
   noise scale, or use the equivalent common-amplification limit. Require
   recovery under the named prior/noise/measure conditions. T48 supplies
   such a conditional proper-law construction, including every positive
   Student nu in its stated residual model.
2. In a growing noisy series, state the nuisance-history class and the
   sampling quantifiers before promising consistency. T53 supplies one
   sharp uniform Gaussian benchmark; T39 and T47 supply other named
   regular repeated-signal results. Extending these beyond their declared
   covariance, subspace and noise assumptions requires a new proof.

Newton II supplies the compatible value in the identifying exact limit.
It does not supply information that the allowed noisy experiment lacks.
This proposed scope keeps consistency as a theorem about an experiment
class, rather than adding latent energy, support or a tuning constant to
the input type. It does not replace L11's wording or strong grade, assert
that these two clauses exhaust a future full-type contract, or close G25.
The forced sort is the failure of the old universal sufficiency implication;
the complete replacement remains to be justified.

### 4 Nov: identifying histories need a separate uncertainty proof (Codex)

T58 constructs a fixed weakening Gaussian history with divergent
Delta_N^2/(N+Delta_N), on which the T57 oscillation-power law and median
remain consistent but the central interval coverage tends to zero.
Every fixed exponent in that family has such a history. This is a
calibration failure inside an attainable identifying class; it neither
reopens the information impossibility proof nor refutes T57's narrower
repeated-block theorem.

T59 supplies a complete calibrated score-anchor comparison on every
history satisfying that information condition in the stated scalar
common-covariance Gaussian experiment. Its schedule uses the observed
scatter spectrum, without receiving Delta_N, N or latent signals.
The half exponent is forced only within the explicitly declared spectral
power class under the combined ordering/width guarantee. The simpler
T43 spectral Gaussian reference remains available. Full-type L11,
selection of a finite-data law and permitted calibration state remain
unsorted; no new physical input or premise wording is adopted.

T60 supplies the joint counterpart under its fixed common physical
Gaussian covariance and full common-acceleration graph. Arbitrary fixed
directions, sizes and leverage are allowed; the same information scale
is sufficient for a proper joint law with calibrated full-law composite
intervals. This is not a necessity theorem for every joint history or a
new contract over heterogeneous noise/subspace inputs. L11's original
wording and grade stay unchanged.
