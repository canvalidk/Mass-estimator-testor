## The holes, stated before any result

1. **ν_eff's default.** The GUM's fallback for an unstated ν is infinity, the fixed-size Gaussian,
   which fails T30 (`test_t30_fails_for_the_fixed_size_gaussian`). So a bare σ is a real gap in the
   type, not a convention. **Unknown yet.**
2. **Positivity in the size posterior.** The posterior over $v_i$ uses the strong-signal count
   $Q\mid v\sim v\chi^2_{\nu}$, applied to $Q_+$; the sign condition's extra share (O13's ½) is not
   counted. Expected to matter for anti-parallel and near-boundary readings.
3. **Weak signal.** At weak signal the misfit carries information about the signal size as well as
   the noise size; the size posterior ignores it. ours27 (no η) under-covered badly at weak signal;
   whether the exact cut factor repairs that alone is the first study.
4. **The power form.** L21 was ruled as $E_i^{g}$; ours31 uses the exact cut factor, of which the
   power is the first order (T37 (4)). Which is the body is G19, a ruling.

### 3 Nov, cut continuation: normalizing each conditional changes the result (Codex)

T44 (`../definition/theorems/T44.md`) separates two operations that the phrase
"average over the noise posterior" can conceal. This is a normalization and
modular-inference question, not a choice made by coverage scores.

Let the reading-level noise laws be frozen at
$q_Y(dV)=\prod_iq_i(dv_i\mid Q_{+,i})$. For a positive common factor
$\eta_N$ and $A_i(m)=H_i^+-H_i(m)$, write

$$L_Y(m,V)=\exp\!\left[-\frac{\eta_N}{2}
\sum_i\frac{A_i(m)}{v_i}\right],\qquad
Z_Y(V)=\int\pi(dm)L_Y(m,V).$$

Here the power is inside each conditional. At power 1, factor averaging
is the raw ours31/ours32 operation; unchanged ours32 instead powers the
already averaged factor. Neither power placement nor normalization order
commutes with averaging. The conditional-power amplitude result below is
an explicitly different construction.

Averaging factors and then normalizing produces

$$p_{\rm factor}(dm)
=\frac{\pi(dm)\int q_Y(dV)L_Y(m,V)}{\int q_Y(dV)Z_Y(V)}
=\int q_Y^*(dV)\frac{\pi(dm)L_Y(m,V)}{Z_Y(V)},$$

where $q_Y^*(dV)\propto Z_Y(V)q_Y(dV)$. Thus the mass normalizer changes
the effective law of V. Freezing q in the integrand is not by itself enough
to preserve q as the mixing distribution of normalized mass laws.

The genuinely frozen **whole-series** conditional mixture is instead

$$p_{\rm cut}(dm)
=\int q_Y(dV)\frac{\pi(dm)L_Y(m,V)}{Z_Y(V)}.$$

It retains $Z_Y(V)$ inside the integral. This is not a product of separately
normalized per-reading cut marginals. Such a product would generally define
a different operation and would need its own argument. Nor is the curvature
of this mixture generally the average conditional precision at finite N.

**What normalization repairs.** For one reading at increasing aligned
amplitude, each fixed finite $v>0$ gives a concentrating normalized Gaussian
conditional mass law. Averaging those normalized laws under a proper frozen
noise law preserves concentration by bounded convergence, including every
allowed $\nu_{\rm eff}>0$. Hence the single-reading $\nu<1$ obstruction in
T39 is absent for this whole-series cut. T44 also proves the fixed-N
amplitude result under its stated conditions, including a fixed positive
conditional power (or the named powers bounded away from zero). This is
not a claim about arbitrary vanishing powers or all signal-growth schedules.

**The separate break after joining readings.** Use 1D readings and
$\nu_{\rm eff}=\nu$. The frozen law has

$$E[1/V_i\mid Y_i]=w(Q_{+,i})=\frac{\nu}{\nu+Q_{+,i}}.$$

This conditional mean enters T44's joint data-and-cut-state law of large
numbers for the Gaussian weighted scatter; it is not asserted to equal
the curvature of the finite-N mixed mass density. At zero true signal,
standardized independent Gaussian X,Y satisfy

$$E[w(Q_+)XY]
=B_\nu=\frac{1-\nu e^\nu E_1(\nu)}\pi>0,\qquad
E_1(x)=\int_x^\infty e^{-t}\frac{dt}{t}.$$

The sign-dependent positive-domain fit suppresses negative cross products
and creates a preferred line at 45 degrees. T44's nonzero-signal
counterexample takes true mass 2, fixed standardized signal length 0.001
and $\nu=10$, with independent unit Gaussian errors and correct declared
covariance. This names the Gaussian world used for the counterexample;
it does not claim that its marginal noise-size law is the finite-$\nu$
inverse-gamma mixture used by the frozen construction.
The weighted population optimum is a different interior positive mass.
Whenever $N\eta_N\to\infty$, the Gaussian conditional law concentrates at
that wrong line under the joint data-and-cut-state sampling distribution.
Averaging normalized conditionals preserves the wrong limit: bounded
outside probabilities converge under the joint law, and Markov's inequality
transfers that convergence to the mixture conditional on the observed data.
This covers $\eta=1$ and the named positive-limit ours32 power.

Therefore the whole-series normalized cut repairs a fixed-N tail failure
but reintroduces G22's sign-weighted population bias under repeated weak
nonzero signal. Merely locating the mean of the noise precision correctly
does not establish a consistent mass definition. The common mass is joined;
no shared signal-size population is fitted in this counterexample.

**Authority.** L21's current power form uses the misfit at the reading's
best mass. The direct quote in R-2026-09-29-8 is "The readings are evidence.
Just less." The identification with a modular cut/no-feedback condition in
O14's 30 Oct sort is Claude's provisional interpretation, adopted as a
mathematical construction by T37. It is not an independent verbatim blanket
ban on every mass-to-noise feedback operation. R-2026-11-01-1 leaves G18/G19
standing until an argument forces a side.

T44 thus tests a precise frozen-mixture construction without promoting it

### 3 Nov: changing a monotone positive-misfit precision cannot cure the quadratic cut (Codex)
