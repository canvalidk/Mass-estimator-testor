**The probe** (`../met/ours31.py`):

- **Reading level.** Each reading's noise size $v_i\sim\mathrm{IG}(\nu_{{\rm eff},i}/2,\nu_{{\rm eff},i}/2)$: the
  stated σ estimated from $\nu_{\rm eff}$ Gaussian degrees of freedom, precision right on average (T37,

  $Q_{+,i}$, R-2026-09-07-1), the factor is the exact average over the size's posterior,
  $$f_i(m)=\Big(1+\frac{A_i(m)}{\nu_{{\rm eff},i}+Q_{+,i}}\Big)^{-(\nu_{{\rm eff},i}+k_i-1)/2},\qquad A_i(m)=\max_{m>0}H_i-H_i(m).$$

  not a cut posterior of any law the type declares).
- **Unchanged from ours30:** the type, π (half-Cauchy, centre $c$, an input), the law on $m>0$, the
  point (the median), the exact branch.
- **New input:** $\nu_{{\rm eff},i}$ per reading, no default.

## The holes, stated before any result

1. **ν_eff's default.** The GUM's fallback for an unstated ν is infinity, the fixed-size Gaussian,
   which fails T30 (`test_t30_fails_for_the_fixed_size_gaussian`). So a bare σ is a real gap in the
   type, not a convention. **Unknown yet.**
2. **Positivity in the size posterior.** The posterior over $v_i$ uses the strong-signal count
   $Q\mid v\sim v\chi^2_{\nu}$, applied to $Q_+$; the sign condition's extra share (O13's ½) is not
   counted. Expected to matter for anti-parallel and near-boundary readings.
3. **Weak signal.** At weak signal the misfit carries information about the signal size as well as
   the noise size; the size posterior ignores it. ours27 (no η) under-covered badly at weak signal;
   whether the exact cut factor repairs that alone is the first study.
4. **The power form.** L21 was ruled as $E_i^{g}$; ours31 uses the exact cut factor, of which the
   power is the first order (T37 (4)). Which is the body is G19, a ruling.

(Full table, widths included: the script's output.) At strong signal the two agree; in 1D they
are close (there is no misfit to read); in 3D at weak signal with many readings ours31 is far too
narrow. ours30 also under-covers there (0.81–0.88; O14's known weak-signal shortfall at $N=20$), but
by much less.

**The break and the premise it names (hole 3, as predicted).** The size posterior reads a reading's
noise size from its misfit with the strong-signal count, $Q\mid v\sim v\chi^2_\nu$. At weak signal that
law is wrong: fitting the line soaks up noise, so $E[Q]$ falls below $\nu$ (in 3D at null it is
$\nu/2$, Codex's exact value, O14 27 Oct). The posterior then concludes each reading's noise is
*smaller* than stated, raises its precision above 1 (T37 (3)), and twenty over-trusted readings make
the law narrow. **The premise named:** the misfit's law given the noise size depends on the reading's
own signal size, which nothing declares (L10). In standard terms the misfit is not a pivot for the
noise size at weak signal: the signal size is a nuisance parameter of the cut likelihood, and T37's
count is that likelihood's strong-signal limit.

**What ours30 was doing there.** Its numerator $\kappa=\sup_\lambda E[Q_+]$ (O13) answers the same
question conservatively, by the largest misfit any size produces, and its cap stops the precision
rising above 1. Both were patches for the undeclared signal size, which is why they looked forced
"by meaning"; T37 and B12 say they are not the cut posterior of any stated noise.

**Open, not sorted:** how the cut should read the size when the signal size is undeclared. The
standard routes: condition on the reading's own signal statistic (its larger Gram eigenvalue; Codex's
decomposition $Q/v=q(A,W,V)$ in O14 has the joint law), which is per reading and borrows nothing
(L19); or bound over sizes, as O13 did; or integrate over sizes, which L10 forbids without a declared
size law. A sort, Claude's, next.

### 30 Oct (later): the break re-attributed (Claude; a correction to the section above)

Can asked whether the strong-signal under-coverage had gone. It had never been the problem: every
law covers at strong signal. The check that should have come first: the same failing cells (3D,
$N=20$, Gaussian world, 200 series, seed `[3110, 900+i]`) with ours27's raw law (= ours31 at
$\nu_{\rm eff}=\infty$) and ours30 without η:

| signal | ours30 | ours31 ($\nu_{\rm eff}=10$) | ours27 | ours30 without η |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0.850 | 0.650 | 0.575 | 0.630 |
| 0.5 | 0.845 | 0.730 | 0.670 | 0.705 |
| 1 | 0.865 | 0.710 | 0.670 | 0.710 |
| 2 | 0.885 | 0.850 | 0.805 | 0.825 |

**So the size posterior is not the cause**: ours31 does better than ours27 and as well as ours30
without η. The section above blamed the strong-signal count; that was wrong as the explanation (the
count is still only the strong-signal limit, but it is not what breaks). **The break is ours27's,
returned because ours31 has no η**, and the premise it names is the one η was patching: the flat
weight on the latent pairs, step 3's endpoint under L10 ("no size law"), which treats every push as
infinitely large and overstates the precision at weak signal (T18; Lindley 1966, a flat prior on
incidental true values, credit record row 4). That is also why B12 found η is not a cut posterior of
any noise law: **η corrected the latent measure, not the noise.**

**The conflict, located.** L10 (no size law, hence the flat measure), property N (a calibrated
width) and L24 (no rescaling of Σ, which η does in form) do not hold together at weak signal with the
flat measure. It is T36's parked item (the measure on $L_i(m)$) and O6 (where π's shape meets it).
The standard danger here is Gleser & Hwang (1987, `../docs/papers/`): no confidence set of finite
expected size with guaranteed coverage exists in errors-in-variables. Not sorted.

### 2 Nov: the reading weight and η with the truth held still (Claude)

**Can (2 Nov):** "what about our 31 and 32 though?", after the forward model (`../met/forward.py`).
The world knows each reading's noise size $v$, so L21's cut can be checked directly: the reading's
weight should be the precision it actually had, $E[1/v\mid\text{the reading}]$ (T37 (4)). ours31's local
weight is its cut factor's curvature at the reading's best line over the Gaussian's,
$w_{31}=(\nu+k-1)/(\nu+Q_+)$. Preset `../presets/forward_ours31_ours32.yaml` (seed 20261103, 4000 draws per
cell; ν declared 10); `../tests/test_forward.py` pins each line below. Not a coverage study.

- **In its own world (v ~ IG(5, 5)) ours31's weight is the true precision** inside the half-line at
  strong push: $E[w]/E[1/v]=1.000$ in 3D, every decile within 0.04. ours30's capped $g$ in the same
  world gives 0.86 of it in 3D, 0.87 in 2D.
- **Hole 3 (weak signal) measured: small.** At zero push $w$ overstates the precision by 5% in 3D, 2% in
  2D (the fitted line absorbs misfit, so readings look cleaner than they are: Neyman–Scott again, now
  in the size channel). The overstatement sits in the misaligned deciles (lowest decile 0.75 against 0.61).
- **Hole 2 (the sign condition's ½) measured: small.** Near the boundary (angle 85°) at push 4: 0.98–0.99
  in 2D–3D. In 1D, where $\nu=k-1=0$ and only a sign flip moves $w$, the flipped readings are
  overweighted (decile gap up to 0.19): the flip says more about $v$ than the count credits.
- **So the weak-signal break of ours31 is not the reading weight.** An overweight of at most 5% per
  reading cannot make 0.57–0.71 out of 0.95. This confirms the 30 Oct re-attribution (the latent
  measure) by a route that does not run the law.
- **η does not count the size spread twice.** 3D, $N=20$, strong push: ours32's η in its own world has
  mean 0.946 (below 1 in 32%), ours30's η in the fixed-size Gaussian world 0.945 (32%). ours30's η in
  the inverse-gamma world is 0.902 (43%) and the flat η 0.836: without a calibrated reading level, η
  absorbs size spread at the series level. That is a mechanism for ours32's gain at ν_eff = 10, $N=5$
  (1 Nov).
- **η tempers about a third of series at the truth in every correctly specified world** (strong push,
  $N=20$): the clip of a noisy ratio at its fitted point, not a misfit of the model. That is G3/O14's
  finite-$N$ form, the same for ours30 and ours32.
- **At weak signal η is the same whatever the reading level** (3D, $N=20$, push 0: 0.42–0.48 for η30
  and η32 in both worlds): its job there is the latent measure, independent of the noise-size model.

What it does not show: the local weight is the cut factor's curvature at the best line only; the
factor's tails (the T30 behaviour on a misaligned reading) are not checked by it. Calibration of a
weight is not coverage of the law.
