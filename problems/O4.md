### 30 Oct: B05, the routes fail as stated (Codex; no ruling)

The full derivation is in `../definition/theorems/T23.md`, with checks beside T23's
existing test in `../tests/test_theorems.py`.

- **Consistency route: fails for current ours30 under literal L11.** In 1D with
  correct unit Gaussian covariance, true mass 2 and fixed nonzero mean
  $\mu=0.01(2,1)/\sqrt5$, the positive-misfit weights have a nonzero population
  score at the true angle. The proof bounds that score above by -0.048. The
  population optimum is a different acute angle; the Bartlett power tends to a
  positive constant and the law and median concentrate at that wrong mass as N
  grows. Total true acceleration energy is $N\,0.01^2/5\to\infty$. Both the model
  and the world are Gaussian, so R-2026-09-27-12 is untouched. The failed inference
  is that the untempered Gaussian expected-score identity survives data-dependent
  positive-misfit weighting.
- **Fixed-N amplitude route: passes for a class, not a Gaussian characterization.**
  With fixed finite errors and increasing true amplitudes, g and η stay bounded
  away from zero and the law concentrates correctly. The same proof applies to
  non-Gaussian bounded Gaussian-scale mixtures with the declared covariance.
  This narrower limit does not establish unrestricted L11 for those mixtures.
- **Z route: insufficient.** Every radial whitened error density gives a constant
  zero-reading factor. More strongly, T23 constructs an explicit non-Gaussian,
  symmetric unit-variance channel density p for which independent errors
  $p(e_F)p(e_a)$ also have a constant zero-line integral. Thus the 24 Oct
  independence qualification above was incomplete: the valid Herschel–Maxwell
  argument additionally assumes full spherical symmetry of the joint error
  density. The zero-line integral does not supply that assumption.

The old Gaussian/Student numerical comparisons are preserved with their original
numbers; they test examples, not a complete characterization. B05 changes neither
`met/`, the equation, nor any premise or ruling. L13's premise-row wording is left
as requested; its claimed derivation is what this finding checks. O4 remains open
because Gaussian forcing has not been established. No replacement noise law or
tempering is proposed.
