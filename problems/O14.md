# O14: η at finite N

**Status:** open. **Kind:** proof, then the tester. **Last updated:** 2026-10-27.

## The item (lemma list d4, verbatim)

**$\eta$ at finite $N$** (T31). The power form is asymptotically forced; with few readings the raw $J$ is biased low (weak signal at $N=20$ covers 88–89%). A Z-safe small-sample correction would count effective readings, $(\sum_ic_i)^2/\sum_ic_i^2$ with $c_i$ the curvature contributions, never $N$. With several instruments, one $\eta_g$ per noise pair may be what property N asks for (the ours28b calibration theorem: sharing is exact only within classes of equal sensitivity).

derivation, then the tester

## Attempts

- 2026-10-24, Claude (a measurement, not a correction): one noise pair, 1D readings of equal
  signal r, 200 series of 400 readings. $\eta$ averages 0.525 at r = 1 (target 0.5) and 0.823
  at r = 2 (target 0.8), with a spread of 0.09 between series; single series fall as low as
  0.67 at r = 2. The spread comes from J, a sum of squared scores with a heavy tail.
  `../tests/test_theorems.py::test_t18_eta_is_the_inverse_on_long_series`.

- 2026-10-26, Claude (generation session; a candidate, not a correction): **a second noise
  channel, off the line.** η reads the noise along the line, from the readings' disagreement
  about $m$, and is heavy-tailed. Under isotropy of inertia (L22, proposed) the misfit $Q_i$ is
  pure noise, independent of $m$, with $\sum_i(k_i-1)$ degrees of freedom, so
  $\sum_iQ_i/\sum_i\nu_i$ estimates the noise scale. T30 already computes $Q_i$ but only
  discounts with it, capped at 1. Toy (3D, declared $\Sigma$ correct, true scale 1, 400 series
  each; script not kept): r = 5: 0.99 ± 0.21 at N = 20, 0.97 ± 0.05 at N = 400; r = 2: 0.84,
  0.84; r = 1: 0.62, 0.64; r = 0.5: 0.53, 0.54. Biased low at weak signal, because fitting the
  common direction soaks up noise. The bias is a function of r, which the larger eigenvalue
  estimates; the 27 Oct table in `O13.md` (E[Q] against r) is that function for equal channels.
  It needs both isotropies: L22 makes the misalignment pure noise, and L9 carries the
  perpendicular scale over to the along-line one. It learns one scale on the declared shape.
  Absent in 1D, and the 24 Oct measurement above was 1D, so the two channels are not yet
  compared. **Gated by O7:** a null reading's misfit carries noise information, so using it is
  allowed only on the "up to a scale" branch (O7, 27 Oct). Open: E[Q | r] in general and its
  inversion at small N; separating $\sigma_F$ from $\sigma_a$ by leverage; trusting the declared
  shape both ways, which the present cap hedges against.
