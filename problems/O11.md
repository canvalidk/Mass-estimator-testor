# O11: The default centre: L3′ and the units argument

**Status:** open. **Kind:** ruling. **Last updated:** 2026-10-24.

## The item (lemma list d4, verbatim)

L3′, and with it T14. **10 Oct, the units argument, offered.** Premise: the default declaration for mass with instruments $(\sigma_F,\sigma_a)$ and for inverse mass with $(\sigma_a,\sigma_F)$ are one declaration read backwards (the symmetry of m and $\mathrm m^{-1}$, units-and-axes record §3). Then $c(\sigma_F,\sigma_a)=1/c(\sigma_a,\sigma_F)$, and with $c=k\,\sigma_F/\sigma_a$ from L3, $k^2=1$. In general: **$c$ may depend only on declared quantities that invert under the swap, and any swap-invariant dimensionless factor is 1.** So $c$ cannot depend on $\rho$, $k$, $d$ or $N$, and one noise pair, correlated or not, forces $c=s$ (T14's value). Where it stops: several instruments leave every weighted geometric mean $\prod_g s_g^{w_g}$ (T20 stands); anisotropic $\Sigma_F,\Sigma_a$ leave any $h$ of the eigenvalues of $\Sigma_a^{-1/2}\Sigma_F\Sigma_a^{-1/2}$ with $h(1/\lambda)=1/h(\lambda)$, of which the determinant ratio $(\det\Sigma_F/\det\Sigma_a)^{1/2d}$ is the unique power mean.
