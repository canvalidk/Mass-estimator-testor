# O11: The default centre: L3′ and the units argument

**Status:** open. The several-instrument and anisotropic cases sorted 9 Nov (Claude, provisional): declared, not derived. **Kind:** sort (Claude) and a question about the goal; was "ruling" until R-2026-11-07-1. **Last updated:** 2026-11-09.

## The item (lemma list d4, verbatim)

L3′, and with it T14. **10 Oct, the units argument, offered.** Premise: the default declaration for mass with instruments $(\sigma_F,\sigma_a)$ and for inverse mass with $(\sigma_a,\sigma_F)$ are one declaration read backwards (the symmetry of m and $\mathrm m^{-1}$, units-and-axes record §3). Then $c(\sigma_F,\sigma_a)=1/c(\sigma_a,\sigma_F)$, and with $c=k\,\sigma_F/\sigma_a$ from L3, $k^2=1$. In general: **$c$ may depend only on declared quantities that invert under the swap, and any swap-invariant dimensionless factor is 1.** So $c$ cannot depend on $\rho$, $k$, $d$ or $N$, and one noise pair, correlated or not, forces $c=s$ (T14's value). Where it stops: several instruments leave every weighted geometric mean $\prod_g s_g^{w_g}$ (T20 stands); anisotropic $\Sigma_F,\Sigma_a$ leave any $h$ of the eigenvalues of $\Sigma_a^{-1/2}\Sigma_F\Sigma_a^{-1/2}$ with $h(1/\lambda)=1/h(\lambda)$, of which the determinant ratio $(\det\Sigma_F/\det\Sigma_a)^{1/2d}$ is the unique power mean.

### 9 Nov: past one noise ratio the centre is declared (Claude, provisional; from T49 and E9)

The item's last clause asks for the several-instrument and anisotropic cases. Two facts bear on them.

- T49 (4 Nov, on main; Codex, not checked by Claude): a default recomputed from the series' declarations moves when an
  exact-zero reading from another instrument is appended, against Z at a fixed supplied $\pi$. So no rule inside the
  function may read the centre off the series' instruments. The same holds for the weights $w_g$ in "every weighted
  geometric mean": they could only come from which instruments the series happens to contain, or from the data
  (against T20).
- E9 (9 Nov, on its branch; `O6.md`, 9 Nov): the one canonical candidate, the normalized curve volume of the declared
  geometries, has no reciprocal centre for noise ratios 1, 2, 3, so it lies outside L4's family for every $c$ (checked
  by Claude). An anisotropic instrument is several ratios in this sense (E9 §2).

So the units argument ends where the item says it stops. For one noise ratio, $c=s$ is the caller's convention when
nothing is known of the object, with the 10 Oct argument fixing its constant. For several ratios the centre is a
declared number and no default is derived. This agrees with `../TASK.md` §6 and with `met/full.py`, where `c` is
required. Still open, and a question about the goal (`../definition/bounds.md`): whether "no knowledge of the object"
is something the definition promises a default for at all.
