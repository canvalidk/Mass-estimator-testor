# O13: Readings that contradict each other

**Status:** open. **Kind:** ruling. **Last updated:** 2026-10-26.

## The item (lemma list d4, verbatim)

**Contradiction between readings.** Two readings declared separate that contradict each other about $m$ collapse the law onto the stronger with unbounded confidence; the law cannot see the contradiction, the data-only resolution (property N) can. Same family as the $\sqrt N$ tilt. **Partly answered by T31:** contradiction about $m$ is exactly what $\eta$ reads, and it widens the law; the resolution still reports it.

## What would close it

ruling on whether anything more is owed

  c = 1 fixed. It exposes absolute correction cost separately from the normalized law.
  This single-reading investigation informs what disagreement the complete result retains;
  it does not settle the original question about contradiction between separate readings.

    95% interval [55.3152, 7957.94]. The independent geometric diagnostic, not a mass
    readout, supplies the cost and the boundary classification.
  - For the 30-degree and perpendicular pairs, once Q exceeds nu the tempered law stops
    changing as both SDs shrink, while the positive correction cost grows as SD^-2.
    Candidate-cloud sizes and the point can still change. The study records those
    quantities separately rather than calling a frozen law proof of adequate behaviour.
  - Open: what relationship between absolute correction cost and the complete mass
    result is required by the premises? No new rejection gate, noise-inflation rule or
    pass/fail condition is adopted. These deterministic cases have no planted mass;
    their corrections are fitted compatible corrections, not recovered actual errors.
