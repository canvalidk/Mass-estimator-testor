## The item (lemma list d4, verbatim)

**Contradiction between readings.** Two readings declared separate that contradict each other about $m$ collapse the law onto the stronger with unbounded confidence; the law cannot see the contradiction, the data-only resolution (property N) can. Same family as the $\sqrt N$ tilt. **Partly answered by T31:** contradiction about $m$ is exactly what $\eta$ reads, and it widens the law; the resolution still reports it.

**Opposite control.** For F=(1,0), a=(-1,0), write epsilon=sigma² and

$$h_c(m)=\frac{c}{c^2+m^2},\quad f(m)=\frac{m}{1+m^2},\quad

Since f(1/m)=f(m) and h_c(1/m)/m²=h_(1/c)(m), their normalizers agree and

$$p_c(1/m)/m^2=p_{1/c}(m).$$

### 27 Oct pressure check: exact null moments and a finite counterexample to the numerator

**Codex derivation and counterexample; no ruling or change to ours29.** This checks the
candidate immediately above. The scope is independent isotropic Gaussian errors in the two
channels, the full k-dimensional subspace, k = 1, 2, 3. Unequal channel SDs are allowed:
write x = F/sigma_F, y = a/sigma_a, so the true positive slope in these coordinates is
m/s, where s = sigma_F/sigma_a. No claim about general cross-channel covariance is made.
Checks are in `tests/test_positivity_candidate.py`; they use deterministic integrals and
independent latent-vector fits, with no random study or planted information passed to ours29.

**Outcome.** The proposed constants are positive and give the desired bounded tail on
anti-parallel pairs. They are **not** the supremum of the expected positive-domain misfit
over the permitted positive scalar-mass experiments. A single dimension-only numerator
cannot both have that supremum meaning and preserve every acute-pair weight for k >= 2.
The mis-stated step is the extrapolation from equal whitened channel strengths to all
allowed experiments; the sign information remains in the readings, not an extra input.

#### 1. Exact zero-signal expectations

Here "null" means the **random zero-signal experiment** x,y independent N(0,I_k), not an
exactly zero observed pair. Put A=|x|^2, B=|y|^2, C=x dot y, T=A+B and
Delta=sqrt((A-B)^2+4C^2). Then Q=(T-Delta)/2, while Q_+=Q for C>0 and min(A,B)
for C<=0 (T32).

| k | E Q | E(Q_+-Q) | E Q_+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | 1/2 - 1/pi | 1/2 - 1/pi = 0.181690114 |
| 2 | 2 - pi/2 | pi/4 - 1/2 | 3/2 - pi/4 = 0.714601837 |
| 3 | 1 | 1 - 2/pi | 2 - 2/pi = 1.363380228 |

**Derivation.** Rotating the two columns of the central Gaussian k-by-2 matrix leaves
its distribution unchanged. Conditional on its eigenvalues, its Gram eigenvector angle
theta is uniform, independent of T and Delta. Thus
A-B=Delta cos(2 theta), 2C=Delta sin(2 theta). Half the angles have C<0 and
E|cos(2 theta)|=2/pi. Consequently

$$E Q=k-\tfrac12 E\Delta,\qquad
  E(Q_+-Q)=E\Delta\left(\tfrac14-\tfrac1{2\pi}\right).$$

For k=1 the Gram matrix has rank one, Delta=T and E T=2. For k>1, changing the central
Gaussian matrix variables to its Gram eigenvalues gives the factor
exp(-T/2) (lambda_+ lambda_-)^((k-3)/2) (lambda_+-lambda_-).
With rho=Delta/T and the eigenvalue Jacobian T/2, the joint density separates into
T^(k-1) exp(-T/2) and rho(1-rho^2)^((k-3)/2). Hence E T=2k and rho has normalized
density (k-1)rho(1-rho^2)^((k-3)/2), yielding E Delta=pi at k=2 and 4 at k=3.
These substitutions give the table. An independent check uses sign symmetry to obtain
E Q_+=(E Q+E min(A,B))/2, with
E min(A,B)=integral_0^infinity P(chi_k^2>t)^2 dt.
`test_null_expectations_from_independent_radial_integrals` checks these integrals.

#### 2. A permitted finite experiment defeats each proposed constant

Take independent xi,zeta ~ N(0,I_k) and true collinear positive means

$$x=R e_1+\xi,\qquad y=b e_1+\zeta,\qquad R,b>0.$$

For every finite R,b this is an ordinary scalar-mass experiment with correctly declared
noise and true m/s=R/b. Couple the same errors as R grows. Then A/R^2 tends to 1 and
C/R tends to y_1. When y_1>0, the smaller Gram eigenvalue tends to B-y_1^2; when y_1<0,
Q_+=min(A,B) tends to B. Since 0<=Q_+<=B and E B is finite, dominated convergence gives

$$\lim_{R\to\infty} E Q_+=k-1+h(b),\qquad
 h(b)=E[(b+Z)_-^2]=(1+b^2)\Phi(-b)-b\phi(b)>0,$$

where Z is standard normal and z_-=max(-z,0). The expectation of Q itself tends to k-1,
so the extra positivity cost tends to h(b). This already disproves kappa_k=k-1 for
k>=2 when the supremum ranges over all allowed strengths and mass/noise ratios.

There is also an explicit **finite** bound, avoiding reliance on a limiting experiment.
Let U=x/sqrt(A), and M=B-max(U dot y,0)^2. This is the cost of leaving x fixed and fitting
y on its nonnegative ray, so Q_+<=M<=B (endpoints are infima). For C>0,
AB-C^2=AM=Q(A+B-Q), and therefore

$$0\le M-Q_+=\frac{M(B-Q)}{A+B-Q}\le\frac{B^2}{A}.$$

For C<=0 the difference is (B-A)_+, bounded by B^2/A too. Conditional on U,

$$E[M\mid U]=k-1+b^2(1-U_1^2)+h(bU_1)\ge k-1+h(b),$$

because U_1<=1 and h is decreasing: h'(z)=2[z Phi(-z)-phi(z)]<0. Split on
A>=R^2/4. On that event the cost difference is at most 4B^2/R^2; on its complement
it is at most B. Independence of x and y and
P(A<R^2/4)<=P(xi_1<-R/2)=Phi(-R/2) now give

$$E Q_+\ge k-1+h(b)
 -\frac{4[(k+b^2)^2+2(k+2b^2)]}{R^2}
 -(k+b^2)\Phi(-R/2).$$

#### 4. The inner fork is now a demonstrated incompatibility

Let S_k be the supremum of E Q_+ over **all** allowed positive collinear Gaussian means,
including their mass/noise ratio. The limit above, followed by b decreasing to zero, gives
S_k>=k-1/2. At the true positive ratio the unfitted residual cost has expectation k,
and Q_+ is its infimum, so S_k<=k. Thus

$$k-\tfrac12\le S_k\le k.$$

These are bounds, **not a proof of the exact supremum**. For k>=2 they already contradict
the demand to retain every acute-pair weight with one dimension-only numerator. Choose an
acute pair with Q_+=Q=2k. Both caps are inactive; the old weight is (k-1)/(2k), while
S_k/(2k)> (k-1)/(2k). `test_supremum_numerator_cannot_preserve_all_acute_weights`
pins this obstruction. Also sup E(Q_+-Q)>=1/2, exceeding every null extra-cost value
in section 1: the earlier separate-factor suggestion cannot justify its positivity
numerator by claiming the null is the supremum either.

No new numerator or factorization is selected. What survives is the positive-cost tail

unchanged acute weights. Resolving that mathematical fork is a new derivation task,
