## The item

`definition/type.md` says the definition returns a law of mass on $(0,\infty)$ and a point. Every probe
from ours21 to ours33 is built to that output, and four open items live inside it: the starting law's
shape and centre (O6, O11), the point (O2), and what the quoted width promises on noise (O15, G28).

E13 (Can, 21 Nov; branch `explore/e13-unnormalized-evidence`) proposes a different output: no starting
law and no normalisation. What is returned is the curve $K(m)$ (T73), sets of candidate masses that
keep a stated promise and are allowed to be unbounded (T75), the leftover beside them, and a
composition of curves (T74). The all-zero experiment returns the flat curve and every positive mass.

R-2026-11-22-1: this direction is pursued on main by harvesting, piece by piece, until it lands.
Nothing on the law-and-point line is struck in advance: `equation.md`, ours33 and the premise list
stand as they are, and a page changes when a harvested piece forces the change.

## What is forced about the kind of output (22 Nov; T80; R-2026-11-22-2)

From three things, two of them on the premise list (every σ is positive; no signal is promised; the quoted
uncertainty is a true statement for every true mass), the quoted uncertainty is a set of masses that can
be unbounded. An interval that is always finite fails however fast it widens. The one alternative is
truth on average over a starting law, which is what the law-and-point output states; it needs the
starting law to be a fact about the object, and nothing on the pages supports that. Sorted to the set,
provisional. The set has four shapes (an interval, "at least", "at most", every positive mass), decided
by which channel can be told from zero, and passes between them continuously in the noise angle. Where
every positive mass is kept the point's value does not matter, though a point is still returned for
type safety (Can, R-2026-11-22-2). So the kind of output E13 returns is
no longer only a direction being tried: it is what those three premises ask for. Which set is not forced
by this, and is the item below.

## What would close it

One of two things. The E13 output written as one body (which statistic, which null, which weight,
what is quoted) that is reported against every row of `GOAL.md` §4 as passes, fails or unknown, with
what replaces each row it contradicts. Or a row that the E13 output cannot meet and the law-and-point

## The point on E13: two zero-padding endpoints, and tropical composition of $\arg\min K$ (22 Nov, Can with Antigravity; provisional)

Can noted an under-explored consequence of the earlier zero-reading drift results: when appending
exact-zero (`0/0`) readings moves a point by diminishing steps and converges onto a limit, starting
directly at that endpoint makes `0/0` readings stop moving the point. Two distinct endpoints already
exist in the repo:

1. **The `T10` cloud-readout endpoint** (proper-law line, fixed instrument ratio $s$; `O2.md`,
   `tests/test_readout_weight.py`, `docs/noise_vs_signal/ours27_candidate_size_and_null_readings_2026-09-28.md` §3):
   appending `0/0` readings leaves the proper law $p(m)$ unchanged (`T24`) and drives the cloud weight
   to $(1+m^2/s^2)^{-1/2}$, converging on the circular mean of the noise angle $\theta=\arctan(m/s)$,
   $$\widehat m_\infty^{(s)}(p)
     = \frac{\int m\,(1+m^2/s^2)^{-1/2}\,p(m)\,dm}{\int (1+m^2/s^2)^{-1/2}\,p(m)\,dm}
     = s\,\frac{E_p[\sin\theta]}{E_p[\cos\theta]}.$$
   Starting here makes same-$s$ `0/0` readings exact fixed points, is always finite on every proper
   law (`L2`, no moments needed), respects `L3`, `T5` and `L19` — **but** loses finite-noise point
   composition (`L5`/`L14`: each body gets its own $m$-dependent weight, e.g. equal-probability
   $(m_1,m_2)\in\{(1,1),(3,3)\}$ at $s=1$ gives $1.618+1.618=3.236$ for the parts against $3.075$ for
   the lump $\{2,6\}$) and keeps a reference-scale anchor ($s$: different-$s'$ zeros have a different
   endpoint and $c\neq s$ returns $R(c)\neq c$; replacing $s$ by $c$ fixes `Z` and returns $c$ on
   all-zero, but two identical laws $p(m)$ with different $c$ give different points, e.g.
   $\tfrac12\delta_1+\tfrac12\delta_4$ gives `2.000` at $c=2$ and `1.766` at $c=1$, against `L17`).

2. **The `B11` unknown-scale endpoint** (`O7.md`, `O14.md` B11): appending `0/0` readings drives the
   median of $\pi(m)K(m)^{-(n/2+a-1)}$ onto the curve minimizer
   $$\widehat m_{\rm Deming} = \arg\min_{m>0} K(m)$$
   (on `(2,1), (1,1)` with $\Sigma=I_2$: `1.5811 → 1.6099 → 1.61801 → 1.618034`, the golden ratio).
   Starting here is bit-for-bit invariant under `0/0` readings from **any** instrument (`T73`),
   requires no starting law $\pi$, and carries no $s$ or $c$ anchor.

**Why #2 is a forcing lead on E13 (not merely elegance).**
- *Integral functionals do not exist on $K(m)$ alone:* by `B16`, $e^{-K(m)/2}$ is non-integrable in
  both $dm$ and $d\ln m$ on every finite series, so without $\pi$ neither the median, the geometric
  mean nor the circular mean is defined; only shape / level-set / extremum functionals of $K$ survive.
- *The 28 Oct Jensen no-go disappears under `T74` infimal convolution:* under probability-law
  convolution (`O2`, 28 Oct), `L5` requires the arithmetic mean and `L14` the harmonic mean, which
  contradict each other on every spread law. Under `T74`'s infimal convolutions
  $K_{\rm lump}(M)=\inf_{m_1+m_2=M}[K_1(m_1)+K_2(m_2)]$ and
  $K_{\rm force}(M)=\inf_{1/m_1+1/m_2=1/M}[K_1(m_1)+K_2(m_2)]$, whenever $K_1,K_2$ attain unique
  interior minima $m_1^*,m_2^*\in(0,\infty)$, the sum $K_1(m_1)+K_2(m_2)\ge K_1(m_1^*)+K_2(m_2^*)$
  attains equality iff $m_1=m_1^*$ and $m_2=m_2^*$. Therefore
  $$\arg\min_M K_{\rm lump}(M) = m_1^* + m_2^*,\qquad
    \frac{1}{\arg\min_M K_{\rm force}(M)} = \frac{1}{m_1^*} + \frac{1}{m_2^*}$$
  hold **simultaneously and exactly at finite noise** (clause (b) on composed curves; level-set
  midpoints fail already on `T74 (2)` Gaussian curves).
- It also respects `L3`, `T5`, `L20`, `L19`, `L23` (parametrisation-free), exact-branch calibration
  `L7`, and as the weighted-TLS point attains the sharp `L11`/`B27` rate $\Delta^2/(D+\Delta)\to\infty$.

**Provisional status and open walls (briefed as `../briefs/B28-tropical-point-uniqueness.md`).**
Can's posture (22 Nov): *"if we keep our hand on the left wall, there shouldn't be a thing called a
different path. So to make a choice is to let go. So its always provisional. #2 seems more natural,
pending further justification."* Two things stand before calling #2 forced:
1. **Tropical uniqueness (the `C1–C5` counterpart):** passing `L5` and `L14` on `T74` curves is not

2. **Boundary minima (`0` and `∞`) and ties:** at `0/0` (every mass compatible), `R-2026-11-22-2`

## 24 Nov: "same curve, same result" (the premise the explorations call L17 or reading splitting), sorted (Claude, provisional)

Explorations E14 to E17 lean on one premise under two names. As "curve-only" it is the first condition of E15's
uniqueness theorem; as "reading splitting" it is tested on one reading against n readings $1/\sqrt n$ the size, and it is
the step in E17 that gives the Gaussian curve without the Gaussian being assumed. Stated once: **two series with the same
curve (and the same score variance) get the same result, the set and its width included.** It was sorted before anything
built on it is taken. In plain words:

1. **It is not L17.** L17 as ruled (R-2026-09-28-4) is "same candidate law, same *mass*". Carried to the curve that is
   "same curve, same point", and the curve's least has it (T81). L17 says nothing about the width. T82 already notes this
   ("L17 as ruled speaks of the mass").
2. **For the width, the pages have ruled the other way, twice.** R-2026-09-30-3: "the property to drop is that the law
   carries its own uncertainty"; the noise measure lives beside the law, and the width may be computed from each reading's
   own residual (R-2026-09-30-5). That is `TASK.md` §3's own worked example of a wrong premise. And R-2026-09-29-9: the same
   data under a different declaration of readings need not give the same law ("1 3D readings is not the same as 3 1D
   readings"); how readings are grouped, and their window, are declared (L18; R-2026-10-10-4). The record of that night says
   why it had looked "too elementary to be wrong": under the plain Gaussian factor regrouping was an identity, and "it stops
   being one as soon as anything reading-level exists". The same sentence holds here with "discount" for "reading-level".
3. **It is equivalent to having no discount, on results already on main.** Two readings far apart and fifty honest ones can
   have one curve and one score variance (T82 (2)), so a rule that reads only those cannot believe the first less. Both
   discount rules move under the idealised split (T82 (8)). So the premise does not support the version without a weighting
   from outside; it is that version, said another way.
4. **It is not "how the experimenter cut the window does not matter".** The idealised split has no scatter among its parts.
   A real window cut into sub-windows has, and then the Gaussian rule's own answer moves with no discount in play: a body
   of mass 2 at a steady 5 noise units, read once or in 50 sub-windows with the same total information, has its point moved
   by 0.036 rad at the median (the whole-window point's own error is 0.061), its error 16% larger and its set 21% wider;
   both sets hold the true mass at their level (0.94, 0.95). How readings are cut is an input on every line, which is what
   the type already says.
5. **What it forces where it is assumed** (E17's second structure theorem, read, not checked): with results that add and are
   smooth at a zero reading, a reading that equals its equal parts has a quadratic carried state, which on one instrument
   is the Gaussian curve. The condition has the form of the stability characterisation of the Gaussian (Pólya 1923:
   a law that is unchanged when two draws are added and divided by $\sqrt2$ is the bell), here on the log-density, and it
   is of the same family as Gauss's 1809 route from the mean: assume how repeated readings combine and the bell follows.
   So "the Gaussian without assuming it" assumes it in this form.

**Sort.** For the point it is L17 and it stands. For the set it is not a premise: it is R-2026-09-30-3's dropped property
carried to the curve, and it contradicts R-2026-11-17-3, which stands (R-2026-11-24-2). Provisional, like every premise.

**What follows for the harvest.**

- What that something can be is the open design question, and E17 lists three (read, not checked): a count of readings,
  which cannot be continuous at a zero reading; a carried state that adds and is not quadratic, which is a shape for each
  reading's error; or the readings themselves. T82 (8)'s "two of three" is the same list from the other side.
