# O2: the readout

**Status:** open; the main breakthrough the definition needs.
**Owner of the next move:** anyone. **Rulings needed:** continuity at the all-zero experiment (see Questions).
**Last updated:** 2026-10-27.

## The problem in one paragraph

The law of mass (T8) is settled up to O6. The point reported from it is not. The current
readout, the ratio of mean sizes over each reading's candidate cloud (T10), satisfies the
composition laws but changes when a reading that carries no information is appended, and it
returns the starting law's centre at the all-zero experiment only when that centre is the
instrument ratio. What is wanted is a readout that composes, ignores null readings, and
gives a value at the all-zero experiment; or a proof that no readout does all three, after
which a ruling drops one requirement.

## Facts that constrain the search

- The all-zero answer, whatever readout, depends neither on N nor on the $\Sigma_i$ (Z plus
  order-free combination, T12).
- Under the best-guess repair the law tends to $\pi$ as the data shrink while the weight is
  homogeneous of degree one in the data; that is why its limit depends on direction. Any
  candidate built from a weight homogeneous in the data will have the same defect.
- The law itself is Z-exact (T24) and gives median c at all-zero. So any readout that is a
  functional of the law alone passes R-b and R-c but, by the uniqueness theorem, fails R-a
  unless it is a ratio of means of two sizes.
- The identity $\widehat m=E[\alpha m]/E[\alpha]$ (d2 step 5): the cloud readout is the mass
  averaged with weight proportional to how hard each candidate accelerates. The weight is
  what cancels the $1/\alpha$ that makes $E[m]$ infinite (T19). A replacement must keep
  $E[m]$ finite, which rules out plain posterior means.
- 24 Oct, Claude's observation, not checked beyond the one run: at c = s the cloud readout
  and the median agree to $10^{-13}$ on the *original* series and differ only after null
  padding. If that holds in general, the Z failure of the cloud readout is entirely the null
  readings' noise-sized clouds entering the stacked length.

## Prior-return law and the separate cloud readout (Codex, 26 Oct)

`presets/inspection_prior_paths.yaml` supplies a nonzero control: F=(1,0), a=(0,1),
equal independent SD sigma, s=1, and an explicitly varied half-Cauchy centre c.
Its correction cost is 1/sigma² at every mass, so its normalized law is exactly
pi_c. Its conditional mean is (m,1)/(1+m²), with covariance sigma² I/(1+m²).
Consequently A(m)=kappa_sigma/sqrt(1+m²). The factor kappa_sigma cancels in the
readout. This has the same mass dependence as the all-zero isotropic cloud, whose
A(m) is a different constant times 1/sqrt(1+m²).

The readout in both cases is therefore

$$R(c)=\frac{\int_0^\infty m\,h_c(m)/\sqrt{1+m^2}\,dm}
                  {\int_0^\infty h_c(m)/\sqrt{1+m^2}\,dm}
       =\frac{B(1/c)}{B(c)},\qquad
B(c)=\int_0^1\frac{c\,du}{c^2+(1-c^2)u^2},$$

where h_c=c/(c²+m²). Equivalently,

$$R(c)=\begin{cases}
c\,\operatorname{acosh}(1/c)/\arccos(c),&0<c<1,\\
1,&c=1,\\
c\,\arccos(1/c)/\operatorname{acosh}(c),&c>1.
\end{cases}$$

At c = 0.1, 1, 10 the computed points are 0.203533525, 1, 4.91319551, while the
law's mathematical median is respectively 0.1, 1, 10. Independent direct mass
integrals, a Rice/angle readout and reciprocal pairing check the closed form in
`tests/test_inspection_prior_reference.py`; `tests/test_inspection_prior_paths.py`
checks the implemented operation. The point is independent of common SD here.

This isolates the already known O2 mechanism: returning the supplied law does not
make the cloud readout return its centre. The data can carry candidate-size
information beyond the normalized mass law. Whether this nonzero constant-evidence
reading counts as null under Z's intended scope is not decided here; no new premise
or repair is adopted.

## 27 Oct (evening): L12 checked; where the Z failure lives (Claude, pressure; provisional)

Per candidate $X=mY$, so T10's readout is the law tilted by $A(m)=E[Y\mid m]$:
$\widehat m=\int mA\,p\,dm/\int A\,p\,dm$. $A$'s scale cancels; only its $m$-dependence acts.
`../tests/test_readout_weight.py` has both results below.

**1. The series join (T9, L12) is second order, and no rung fixes it.**
- *Composition does not fix it.* In both composition experiments the per-reading sizes being
  joined are proportional: lumping is at common acceleration ($X_{1,i}=m_1Y_i$,
  $X_{2,i}=m_2Y_i$), common force at common force. So every symmetric join homogeneous of
  degree 1 passes L5 and L14: $\ell_1$, $\ell_2$, $\ell_\infty$ alike.
- *No rung bears on it* (`../definition/ladder.md` §3). R4's force sum joins forces on one
  target at one time, which is sources, not readings.
- *L12's "one joining rule" is false inside a reading once the window is in the type.* T33
  makes a reading's force and acceleration time averages over $\Delta t_i$, so sub-intervals
  join by a duration-weighted vector sum, while components join by the Euclidean norm. Two
  joins inside one reading; T9's support picks the component one without saying why.
- *It barely matters.* On the STATE.md series scaled to weak signal (scale 0.05, s = 2), the
  readouts are 2.4075 ($\ell_1$), 2.4082 ($\ell_2$ = T9 = ours29's 2.4083), 2.4090 ($\ell_4$),
  2.4099 ($\ell_\infty$). Ten null readings move each by −0.0098, −0.0098, −0.0093, −0.0080.
- **Call: unknown yet, low stakes.** Not smuggling (it decides no mechanical fact), not an
  input (no instrument sheet supplies a join), not forced. It is not where O2 is blocked.
  Parked until the weight question below is settled, since it only shapes $A$.

**2. Null readings replace the weight by a function of the instrument alone.**
- A null reading's candidate cloud given $m$ is $N(0,v(m)I)$ with
  $v(m)=1/(1/\sigma_a^2+m^2/\sigma_F^2)$, so its size goes as $(1+m^2/s^2)^{-1/2}$. That shape
  is fixed by $\Sigma$, centred at $s$, and the same for every join.
- Appending null readings leaves the law unchanged (T24) and drags $A$ toward that shape.
  With 1000 of them the readout *is* the law tilted by it: 2.3931 against 2.3930 (scale 0.05),
  2.0827 against 2.0826 (scale 0.02). Every move is toward $s$.
- This is one mechanism behind three failures already in this file: Z (T29), the all-zero
  value $R(c)\ne c$ for $c\ne s$, and Codex's perpendicular reading. In each, the weight
  carries a second centre, $s$, from the noise model, besides $\pi$'s $c$.

**3. Provisional sort: outcome 3, and the mis-stated premise is the domain of T21's "law of
the two joined sizes".** L17 (ruled 28 Sept) says the readout's score cannot read the declared
instrument facts. The cloud readout satisfies L17's letter, since it depends on the candidate
law only, but on a null reading the candidate law *is* the instrument: $\Sigma$ alone sets its
$m$-dependence. So the letter (same candidate law, same mass) and the reason (it cannot read
the instrument) come apart exactly where O2 fails. Under `../TASK.md` §4 the reason is kept,
and what is mis-stated is what T21 and T4 average over: the full posterior cloud, noise part
included, instead of what the readings support about the true sizes.

**What any repair must meet, and why it is not yet in hand.**
- *The weight does essential work.* The law has no mean (T19); $A$'s decay, about $1/m$ in the
  tail, is what makes the readout finite. A readout that is a functional of the law of $m$
  alone loses that, and by the uniqueness theorem fails L5 or L14 (the median row above).
- *The obvious signal-only weight is wrong too.* The best-guess size $\lvert E[\mathbf a^\ast\mid m]\rvert$
  is Z-exact (a null reading contributes 0) and decays as $1/m$, but it is homogeneous of
  degree 0 in the data: scaling every reading down leaves its shape unchanged, so near the
  all-zero experiment it reads the noise's *ratio* at full weight. That is the
  direction-dependent limit, 0.546 to 1.830, in the table above.
- So the object sought is a weight that is (i) Z-exact, (ii) decays fast enough for
  finiteness, (iii) loses its shape continuously as the signal goes to zero, so the all-zero
  readout returns $\pi$'s centre. **Unknown yet** whether one exists. If none does, the
  contradiction is between L17's reason and T4's composition, and by TASK §4 one of them is
  mis-stated; on the ladder, L14 is the one resting on a provisional rung (R5).

## 28 Oct: the no-go, and which premise gives (Claude, pressure; provisional)

**1. Under L19 the point's only cross-reading content is the law.** L19 (ruled 28 Sept,
"ironclad" 30 Sept): readings are joined only through the mass they share; nothing inferred
from one reading about signals (size, direction, spread) is used on another. T9's series size
$Y=\oplus Y_i$ joins readings through their signal sizes, and the cloud weight $A(m)=E[Y\mid m]$
reweights every reading's evidence by the others' sizes. `tests/test_readout_weight.py`
shows it directly: null readings leave the law unchanged and move the point by their sizes
alone. That is borrowing. So, under L19, the point is a functional of the law of $m$ (and the
declared inputs).

**2. No law functional meets point-level L5 and L14 on spread laws.** L5 at the point needs
additivity under convolution (the mean), L14 additivity of the inverse (the harmonic mean),
and by Jensen they agree only on point masses; the half-Cauchy has no mean at all (T19). This is
the median row of the table above. So L19, Z and point-level composition are jointly
inconsistent, and by `../TASK.md` §4 one of them is mis-stated.

**3. The one that gives is point-level composition at finite noise.**
- On the ladder, L5 and L14 come from R3 and R5, which are **exact-value** commitments:
  exact masses add; exact inverse masses add under a common force.
- On data, no point meets L5 at finite noise. Bodies 1 and 2 sharing each acceleration
  reading, the lump measured with force $F_1+F_2$, priors that compose ($1+2=3$), 30 series,
  seed 7: the cloud readout's defect is 0.273% (strong), 2.44% (mid), 20.96% (weak); the law's
  median's is 0.273%, 2.62%, 23.25%. Both shrink to zero as the signal grows.
- The ratio of means passes L5 only on laws of sizes from *one* posterior, where the lump's
  force is $X_1+X_2$. A lump is a separate measurement, so data never supply that.
- So what the rungs commit, exact composition, is delivered by T1 on exact inputs and by L11
  in the limit, for any consistent point. As a constraint on the point at finite noise it
  cannot be met by anything, so it cannot be a premise. T3, T4 and T21 stay true of laws of
  sizes and stop constraining the point. `tests/test_point_composition.py`.

**4. What is left, and what it forces.** The point is a functional of the law that is Z-exact
(every law functional is: T24), returns $c$ at the all-zero experiment, respects the swap and
units, and is finite on a law with no mean (T19). Units and the swap leave a family: every
location functional of $\ln m$ that commutes with negation (median, geometric mean, …). That
is a fork. **Sorted as forced, provisionally:** a definition of mass should not depend on the
coordinate its law is read in (L23, proposed: the point commutes with every increasing
reparametrisation, as T31 already requires of $\eta$). Then the point is a quantile, and the
swap makes it the median (T35, tested).

**Candidate: the point is the law's median.** It meets every column of the table above:
Z (exactly), the all-zero value $c$ (for every $c$, not only $c=s$), and composition on data
as well as the cloud does. ours29 already reports it; STATE.md's robot numbers are medians.

**Not shown:**
- L14 on data (only L5 was run).
- Whether the law itself composes: is the lump's law related to the parts' laws?
- The rulings written on the ratio of means: R-2026-09-29-3's combination weight and ruling 3
  of 30 Sept on the quoted width are unaffected as far as I can see; T9, T10 and the stacked
  length become moot.
- E10 d4 reads the measured branch as "inertial mass is one field" of a joint fit. The law's
  median is that field read parametrisation-free, which looks consistent, but it is unchecked
  against E10's wording.

## Questions for Can

   (The undefined record §4 asks this; unanswered.)
2. Is R-c′ (the value is c) a ruling, or does it stay a derivation that a readout may fail

3. O7: does Z cover zeros beside a signal, or only whole null readings? The answer decides

## What would close it

Either a readout with four yeses in the table above, with a composition proof and a test for
Z (law and point) and for the all-zero value; or a proof that R-a, R-b and R-c cannot hold
together, followed by a ruling on which to drop. A candidate that scores well in a study
without an argument is not a move (the "hand on the left wall" rule).
