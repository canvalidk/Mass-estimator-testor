# O2: the readout

**Status:** open; the main breakthrough the definition needs.
**Owner of the next move:** anyone. **Rulings needed:** continuity at the all-zero experiment (see Questions).
**Last updated:** 2026-10-26.

## The problem in one paragraph

The law of mass (T8) is settled up to O6. The point reported from it is not. The current
readout, the ratio of mean sizes over each reading's candidate cloud (T10), satisfies the
composition laws but changes when a reading that carries no information is appended, and it
returns the starting law's centre at the all-zero experiment only when that centre is the
instrument ratio. What is wanted is a readout that composes, ignores null readings, and
gives a value at the all-zero experiment; or a proof that no readout does all three, after
which a ruling drops one requirement.

something else.

## Facts that constrain the search

- The all-zero answer, whatever readout, depends neither on N nor on the $\Sigma_i$ (Z plus
  order-free combination, T12).
- Under the best-guess repair the law tends to $\pi$ as the data shrink while the weight is
  homogeneous of degree one in the data; that is why its limit depends on direction. Any
  candidate built from a weight homogeneous in the data will have the same defect.
- The law itself is Z-exact (T24) and gives median c at all-zero. So any readout that is a
  functional of the law alone passes R-b and R-c but, by the uniqueness theorem, fails R-a
  unless it is a ratio of means of two sizes.
- The identity $\widehat m=E[\alpha m]/E[\alpha]$ (d2 step 5): the cloud readout is the mass
  averaged with weight proportional to how hard each candidate accelerates. The weight is
  what cancels the $1/\alpha$ that makes $E[m]$ infinite (T19). A replacement must keep
  $E[m]$ finite, which rules out plain posterior means.
- 24 Oct, Claude's observation, not checked beyond the one run: at c = s the cloud readout
  and the median agree to $10^{-13}$ on the *original* series and differ only after null
  padding. If that holds in general, the Z failure of the cloud readout is entirely the null
  readings' noise-sized clouds entering the stacked length.

## Prior-return law and the separate cloud readout (Codex, 26 Oct)

`presets/inspection_prior_paths.yaml` supplies a nonzero control: F=(1,0), a=(0,1),
equal independent SD sigma, s=1, and an explicitly varied half-Cauchy centre c.
Its correction cost is 1/sigma² at every mass, so its normalized law is exactly
pi_c. Its conditional mean is (m,1)/(1+m²), with covariance sigma² I/(1+m²).
Consequently A(m)=kappa_sigma/sqrt(1+m²). The factor kappa_sigma cancels in the
readout. This has the same mass dependence as the all-zero isotropic cloud, whose
A(m) is a different constant times 1/sqrt(1+m²).

The readout in both cases is therefore

$$R(c)=\frac{\int_0^\infty m\,h_c(m)/\sqrt{1+m^2}\,dm}
                  {\int_0^\infty h_c(m)/\sqrt{1+m^2}\,dm}
       =\frac{B(1/c)}{B(c)},\qquad
B(c)=\int_0^1\frac{c\,du}{c^2+(1-c^2)u^2},$$

where h_c=c/(c²+m²). Equivalently,

$$R(c)=\begin{cases}
c\,\operatorname{acosh}(1/c)/\arccos(c),&0<c<1,\\
1,&c=1,\\
c\,\arccos(1/c)/\operatorname{acosh}(c),&c>1.
\end{cases}$$

At c = 0.1, 1, 10 the computed points are 0.203533525, 1, 4.91319551, while the
law's mathematical median is respectively 0.1, 1, 10. Independent direct mass
integrals, a Rice/angle readout and reciprocal pairing check the closed form in
`tests/test_inspection_prior_reference.py`; `tests/test_inspection_prior_paths.py`
checks the implemented operation. The point is independent of common SD here.

This isolates the already known O2 mechanism: returning the supplied law does not
make the cloud readout return its centre. The data can carry candidate-size
information beyond the normalized mass law. Whether this nonzero constant-evidence
reading counts as null under Z's intended scope is not decided here; no new premise
or repair is adopted.

## Questions for Can

1. Must the readout be continuous at the all-zero experiment, or is a value there enough?
   (The undefined record §4 asks this; unanswered.)
2. Is R-c′ (the value is c) a ruling, or does it stay a derivation that a readout may fail
   if it keeps everything else?
3. O7: does Z cover zeros beside a signal, or only whole null readings? The answer decides
   whether T25's k-dependence is a defect and which null paddings count as tests.

## What would close it

Either a readout with four yeses in the table above, with a composition proof and a test for
Z (law and point) and for the all-zero value; or a proof that R-a, R-b and R-c cannot hold
together, followed by a ruling on which to drop. A candidate that scores well in a study
without an argument is not a move (the "hand on the left wall" rule).
