## Part I: `L25` Algebraic Structure Theorems (Door-Independent)

### Theorem E17-L1 (Aczél Curve-Addition Uniqueness — Why Unnormalized Curves Add)

Suppose a measurement rule assigns to every series $R$ a pointwise real-valued evidence curve $m \mapsto u_R(m) \in I \subseteq \mathbb R$ ($I$ a real interval with endpoint $e_0 = u_{0/0}(m)$) combining pointwise across independent blocks of one object:
$$u_{R_1 \cup R_2}(m) = F\big(u_{R_1}(m),\; u_{R_2}(m)\big),$$
where $F : I \times I \to I$ is **continuous**, **associative** (`L25 (b)`), **strictly monotone** in each argument on the interior of $I$, and has **identity $e_0$** (`L20`, $F(x, e_0) = F(e_0, x) = x$).

**Proof & consequences:**
1. By **Aczél's Associativity Theorem** (1966, §6.2.2; Craigen & Páles 1989), there exists a continuous strictly monotone bijection $\phi : I \to [0, \infty)$ (or $\mathbb R$) with $\phi(e_0) = 0$ such that
   $$\phi\big(F(x, y)\big) = \phi(x) + \phi(y) \qquad \forall x, y \in I,$$
   unique up to multiplication by a positive constant $c > 0$.
2. **Commutativity is automatic** ($F(x, y) = \phi^{-1}(\phi(x) + \phi(y)) = F(y, x)$).
3. Writing $K_R(m) := \phi(u_R(m))$, block combination is **strictly unnormalized addition**
   $$K_{R_1 \cup R_2}(m) = K_{R_1}(m) + K_{R_2}(m), \qquad K_{0/0}(m) \equiv 0.$$
4. **Door-independent scope:** Nothing here assumes Gaussianity (`L13`). It applies equally to the Gaussian profile curve $K_i(m)$ (`T73`) and to any non-Gaussian radial loss curve $\rho(K_i(m))$ (e.g. Student-$t_\nu$ $\frac{\nu+k_i}{2}\log(1 + K_i(m)/\nu)$). What it **kills** is normalizing into a probability density $p_R(m) = \pi(m) e^{-K_R(m)/2} / Z_R$ (`ours30`–`ours33`), because multiplying $p_{R_1}(m) p_{R_2}(m)$ double-counts $\pi(m)$ (verified in `tests/test_e17_l25_and_g25.py`).

### Theorem E17-L2 (The `L25` Continuity-and-Quadraticity Wall & Exact Price of Door B)

Let $(\mathcal S, \oplus, e)$ be **any** Hausdorff topological commutative monoid carrying the state $S(R) \in \mathcal S$ of a series $R$, with $S(R_1 \cup R_2) = S(R_1) \oplus S(R_2)$ (`L25 (b)`), $S(0/0) = e$ (`L20`), and $r \mapsto S(r)$ **continuous at `0/0`**.

1. **Impossibility of reading counts (2-line proof):** No continuous homomorphism $M : \mathcal S \to \mathbb R$ ($M(x \oplus y) = M(x) + M(y)$, $M(e) = 0$) can equal the reading count $N$, noise dimension count $D = \sum k_i$, or nonzero count $M_{\rm nz}$. *Proof:* If $M(S(y)) = k \ge 1$ for every $y \neq 0$, taking $y \to 0$ gives $k = \lim_{y\to 0} M(S(y)) = M(S(0)) = M(e) = 0$, contradiction. *(This generalizes `T82 (2)` / `E14-6` from `(K, C)` to **every** continuous topological monoid, excluding `AR`, `M_nz`-`GCLR`, and `ours34`'s `t_joint`.)*
2. **Local quadraticity from `L16` + `L20` + $C^2$ smoothness (zero Gaussianity assumed):** Let $\mathcal S = V$ be any topological vector space with $\oplus = +$, so $S(R) = \sum_i \Phi(r_i)$. Fix an instrument covariance $\Sigma \succ 0$ and assume $y \mapsto \Phi(y)$ is $C^2$ at $y = 0$, vanishes at $0$ (`L20`), and is invariant under frame sign flips `L16`: $\Phi(-y) = \Phi(y)$. Differentiating $\Phi(-y) = \Phi(y)$ at $y = 0$ gives $-D\Phi(0) = D\Phi(0) \implies D\Phi(0) = 0$. By Taylor's theorem,
   $$\Phi(y) = \frac{1}{2} D^2\Phi(0)[y, y] + o(\|y\|^2) \qquad (y \to 0).$$
3. **Reading-splitting (`L17`) forces the Gaussian Gram matrix WITHOUT `L13`!** If additionally $\Phi$ is invariant under infinitesimal reading splitting — replacing one reading $y$ by $n$ independent copies of $y/\sqrt n$ leaves $S$ unchanged, i.e., $n\,\Phi(y/\sqrt n) = \Phi(y)$ — then letting $n \to \infty$ gives
   $$\Phi(y) = \lim_{n\to\infty} n\,\Phi\!\left(\frac{y}{\sqrt n}\right) = \frac{1}{2} D^2\Phi(0)[y, y] =: Q(y) \qquad \forall y \in \mathbb R^{2d}.$$
   For an isotropic instrument, `L16` requires $Q(R F, R a) = Q(F, a)$ for all $R \in O(d)$. By Weyl's first fundamental theorem for $O(d)$ (1939), the space of $O(d)$-invariant quadratic forms on $\mathbb R^{2d}$ is **3-dimensional**, spanned by $(\|F\|^2, F \cdot a, \|a\|^2)$ — which is **bijective with the Gaussian evidence curve $K(m)$**. Thus **`L25 (b) + L20 + L16 + C^2-at-0 + reading-splitting` forces the Gaussian curve $K(m)$ without ever stipulating `L13`!** *(And by `E15-1` / `T82 (2)`, kills every disagreement discount.)*
4. **Sharp algebraic price of Door B (including a subtle 1D vs. 2D split for `T75 (4)`):** Consequently, any Door B disagreement-widening procedure must drop at least one of:
   - **Continuous fixed-type `L25 (b)` monoid:** Door B's spherical null `T75 (4)` carries per-reading $(U_i, \|V_i\|)$ (infinite-dimensional multiset state; satisfies weak reading-order invariance `L25 (a)`). Moreover, we prove and verify a sharp dimensional split at `0/0`:
     - **In 1D scalars ($k_i = 1$):** `T75 (4)` is **exact `Z`** yet **discontinuous at `0/0`**! On 5 identical readings `(5, 1)` at $m_0 = 1$, $p = 2/32 = 0.0625$ (kept at 95%), and appending `(0, 0)` keeps $p = 0.0625$, whereas appending ONE tiny reading `(ε, 0)` breaks the sign-flip tie for every $\varepsilon > 0$ (`LR(+v_6) > LR(-v_6)` by $+O(\varepsilon^2)$) and drops $p$ discontinuously to **`2/64 = 0.03125`** (rejected at 95%!).
     - **In 2D/3D vectors ($k_i \ge 2$):** When the base spherical null $T^*$ has a continuous density on $S^{k_i - 1}$ (no atoms at $T_{\rm obs}$), appending $m$ tiny readings of norm $\varepsilon$ perturbs both $T_{\rm obs}$ and every redrawn $T^*$ by $O(m\varepsilon^2)$, so **$p$ IS continuous at `0/0`** (verified in `tests/test_e17_l25_and_g25.py`).
   - **Continuity at `0/0`:** `ours34`'s `t_joint` and `M_nz`-`GCLR` carry the discontinuous count $M_{\rm nz}$.
   - **Reading-splitting (`L17`):** Non-quadratic additive `L25 (b)` curves like $\sum \frac{\nu+k_i}{2}\log(1 + K_i(m)/\nu)$ keep exact `L25 (b)`, `L20`, `L16`, and $C^2$ continuity at `0/0`, at the cost of dropping `L17` and introducing an unpinned tail parameter $\nu$ (`F2`).

## Part II: `G25` Full-Type Non-Gaussian Vector Consistency of `T81`'s Global $\arg\min K$

Let readings $Y_i = \begin{pmatrix} m_0 N_i \alpha_i \\ N_i \alpha_i \end{pmatrix} + e_i$ ($i = 1, \dots, n$) in the full type ($d \in \{1, 2, 3\}$, arbitrary correlated SPD $\Sigma_i$, orthonormal subspaces $N_i \in \mathbb R^{d \times k_i}$) have **independent, arbitrary non-Gaussian errors** satisfying **only**
$$E[e_i] = 0, \qquad \operatorname{Cov}(e_i) = \Sigma_i \succ 0.$$
Let $B_i = \operatorname{diag}(N_i, N_i)$, $\Gamma_i = (B_i^\top \Sigma_i^{-1} B_i)^{-1}$, and $z_i = \Gamma_i^{-1/2} \Gamma_i B_i^\top \Sigma_i^{-1} Y_i = \mu_i + \varepsilon_i \in \mathbb R^{2k_i}$ (`B27`). Direct matrix multiplication (using **only** $\operatorname{Cov}(e_i) = \Sigma_i$, zero Gaussianity!) gives $\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon_i) = I_{2k_i}$.

Write $d_i(m)^2 := \|(I - P_i(m))\mu_i\|^2$, $G_n(m) := \sum_{i=1}^n d_i(m)^2$, $\Delta_n := \sum_{i=1}^n \|\mu_i\|^2$, $D_n := \sum_{i=1}^n k_i$, and centered curve fluctuation $Z_n(m) := K(m) - K(m_0) - G_n(m)$.

**Exact Zero-Bias Identity (for ALL second-moment world laws):**
$$E[K_i(m)] = d_i(m)^2 + 2\mu_i^\top(I - P_i(m)) E[\varepsilon_i] + \operatorname{tr}\!\big((I - P_i(m))\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon_i)\big) = d_i(m)^2 + k_i,$$
hence **$E[K(m) - K(m_0)] = G_n(m) \ge 0$ identically** (zero bias even for arbitrarily skewed, discrete, or multimodal non-Gaussian errors!).

### Theorem E17-G1 (Global Non-Gaussian Vector Consistency & Sharp Rate of `T81` on $[0, \infty]$)

Assume the trace-free quadratic variance of each $\varepsilon_i$ is uniformly bounded:
$$\sup_{A = A^\top,\; \operatorname{tr} A = 0,\; \|A\|_F = 1} \operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_i^\top A \varepsilon_i) \le M_4 < \infty \qquad \forall i.$$
*(Holds automatically with $M_4 = 2$ for Gaussian, $M_4 = 2\frac{\nu-2}{\nu-4}$ for elliptical Student-$t_\nu$ ($\nu > 4$) of any $k_i$, $M_4 = \max(2, \kappa_4 - 1)$ for independent coordinates of kurtosis $\le \kappa_4$, and $M_4 \le 2k_i \kappa_4 \le 6\kappa_4$ for **every** distribution in $d \le 3$ with uniform fourth-moment bound $\kappa_4$.)*

Suppose effective covariances have uniformly bounded condition numbers $\kappa(\Gamma_i) := \lambda_{\max}(\Gamma_i)/\lambda_{\min}(\Gamma_i) \le \kappa_0$. Let $\widehat m_n = \arg\min_{m \in [0, \infty]} K(m)$ (`T81`), $\widehat\phi_n = \arctan(\widehat m_n) \in [0, \pi/2]$, $\phi_0 = \arctan(m_0)$.

**Then:**
1. **Exact global separation on $[0, \pi/2]$:** Writing $\mu_i = \Gamma_i^{-1/2}(\sin\phi_0\,\beta_i;\; \cos\phi_0\,\beta_i)$ gives $\|\mu_i\|^2 \le \lambda_{\min}(\Gamma_i)^{-1}\|\beta_i\|^2$, and
   $$d_i(\phi)^2 = \min_{u \in \mathbb R^{k_i}} \left\|\Gamma_i^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix} \sin\phi_0\,\beta_i - \sin\phi\,u \\ \cos\phi_0\,\beta_i - \cos\phi\,u \end{pmatrix}\right\|^2 \ge \lambda_{\max}(\Gamma_i)^{-1} \|\beta_i\|^2 \sin^2(\phi - \phi_0) \ge \frac{1}{\kappa_i}\|\mu_i\|^2 \sin^2(\phi - \phi_0),$$
   so globally on ALL of $[0, \pi/2]$ (including both ends $0$ and $\infty$),
   $$G_n(\phi) \ge \frac{1}{\kappa_0}\Delta_n \sin^2(\phi - \phi_0) \ge \frac{4}{\pi^2 \kappa_0}\Delta_n (\phi - \phi_0)^2.$$
2. **Uniform derivative-variance bound on $[0, \pi/2]$:** For $U_i(\phi) = \Gamma_i^{-1/2}(\sin\phi\,I_{k_i};\; \cos\phi\,I_{k_i})$, every unit $v \in \mathbb R^{k_i}$ satisfies $\|(\sin\phi\,v;\; \cos\phi\,v)\|^2 \equiv 1$, hence $\|U_i'(\phi)\|_{\rm op} \le \lambda_{\min}(\Gamma_i)^{-1/2}$ and $\|U_i(\phi)^+\|_{\rm op} \le \lambda_{\max}(\Gamma_i)^{1/2}$. Because $P_i'(\phi) = A_i + A_i^\top$ with $A_i = (I - P_i) U_i' U_i^+$ mapping $\operatorname{range}(P_i) \perp \operatorname{range}(I - P_i)$ orthogonally, $\|P_i'(\phi)\|_{\rm op} = \|A_i\|_{\rm op} \le \sqrt{\kappa_i} \le \sqrt{\kappa_0}$ and $\|P_i'(\phi)\|_F^2 = 2\|A_i\|_F^2 \le 2 k_i \kappa_0$. Since $\operatorname{tr}(P_i'(\phi)) = \frac{d}{d\phi}k_i = 0$ and $Z_i'(\phi) = -2\mu_i^\top P_i'(\phi)\varepsilon_i - \varepsilon_i^\top P_i'(\phi)\varepsilon_i$,
   $$\operatorname{Var}(Z_n'(\phi)) \le \sum_{i=1}^n \Big(8\|P_i'(\phi)\mu_i\|^2 + 2M_4\|P_i'(\phi)\|_F^2\Big) \le \kappa_0(8\Delta_n + 4M_4 D_n) =: \sigma_n^2 \qquad \forall \phi \in [0, \pi/2].$$
3. **Sharp global convergence rate via dyadic peeling (zero log factors!):** For any $r > 0$,
   $$E\!\left[\sup_{|\phi - \phi_0| \le r} |Z_n(\phi)|\right] \le \int_{\max(0,\phi_0-r)}^{\min(\pi/2,\phi_0+r)} E[|Z_n'(t)|]\,dt \le 2r\,\sigma_n.$$
   On dyadic shells $S_j = \{\phi \in [0, \pi/2] : 2^j r_0 \le |\phi - \phi_0| < 2^{j+1} r_0\}$ ($j = 0, 1, \dots$), $\widehat\phi_n \in S_j$ forces $\sup_{|\phi-\phi_0| \le 2^{j+1}r_0} |Z_n(\phi)| \ge G_n(\widehat\phi_n) \ge \frac{4}{\pi^2\kappa_0}\Delta_n (2^j r_0)^2$. By Markov's inequality and summing the geometric series $\sum_{j \ge 0} 2^{-j} = 2$,
   $$P\big(|\widehat\phi_n - \phi_0| \ge r_0\big) \;\le\; \sum_{j=0}^\infty \frac{2(2^{j+1} r_0)\,\sigma_n}{\frac{4}{\pi^2\kappa_0}\Delta_n (2^j r_0)^2} \;=\; \frac{2\pi^2 \kappa_0\,\sigma_n}{\Delta_n\,r_0} \;=\; \frac{2\pi^2 \kappa_0^{3/2}\sqrt{8\Delta_n + 4M_4 D_n}}{\Delta_n\,r_0}.$$
   Therefore **`T81`'s unconstrained global minimizer $\widehat m_n$ on $[0, \infty]$ attains the sharp `B27` threshold $\Delta_n^2 / (D_n + \Delta_n) \to \infty$ with the exact rate $|\widehat\phi_n - \phi_0| = O_p\!\big(\sqrt{M_4 D_n + \Delta_n}/\Delta_n\big)$** for all finite-4th-moment non-Gaussian world errors! *(Closes the open lines of `definition/proof.md` lines 89–90 and `problems/O19.md` line 637.)*

### Theorem E17-G2 (Arbitrary Unbounded Instrument Ratios $s_i \in (0, \infty)$: Layer-Cake/Doob Bound & Global Ray Counterexample)

**Lemma (Layer-Cake + Doob Maximal Inequality for Monotone Weights):**
Let $X_1, \dots, X_n$ be independent mean-zero random variables with finite $L_q$ norms ($q \in (1, 2]$), and let $w_i : T \to [0, 1]$ ($i = 1, \dots, n$) be continuous non-decreasing functions on a real interval $T$. Then
$$\left\|\sup_{t \in T} \left|\sum_{i=1}^n w_i(t)\,X_i\right|\right\|_{L_q} \;\le\; \frac{q}{q - 1}\left(C_q \sum_{i=1}^n E[|X_i|^q]\right)^{1/q}, \qquad (C_2 = 1,\; C_q = 2 \text{ for } 1 < q < 2).$$

*Proof (3 lines):* Write $w_i(t) = \int_0^1 \mathbf 1\{w_i(t) \ge u\}\,du = \int_0^1 \mathbf 1\{t \ge t_i(u)\}\,du$ where $t_i(u) = \inf\{t \in T : w_i(t) \ge u\}$. Move the supremum inside the integral, apply Minkowski's integral inequality in $L_q$, and note that for each fixed $u \in (0, 1)$, as $t$ varies over $T$ the sum $\sum_i \mathbf 1\{t \ge t_i(u)\} X_i$ sweeps only the partial sums of a deterministic permutation $\pi_u$ of $\{X_i\}$. Doob's $L_q$ martingale maximal inequality + von Bahr–Esseen (1965) bounds each slice uniformly by $\frac{q}{q-1}(C_q \sum E|X_i|^q)^{1/q}$, independent of $u$ and of the breakpoints $t_i(u)$. $\square$

**Consequences for arbitrary unequal instrument ratios $s_i \in (0, \infty)$ (and arbitrary correlated $k_i = 1$ SPD $\Gamma_i$, where $\theta_i(m)$ sweeps a single convex arc of length $\arccos(-\rho_i) < \pi$):**

1. **Exact Global Chord Lower Bound:** Let $w_i = \frac{m_0 s_i}{m_0^2 + s_i^2}$ (`B27`'s local speed), $J_n = \sum w_i^2 \|\mu_i\|^2$, and $\rho(m, m_0) = \frac{|m - m_0|}{\max(m, m_0)} \in [0, 1]$. For **every** $m \in [0, \infty]$ and **every** $s_i \in (0, \infty)$ (setting $x = m/m_0$, $u = s_i/m_0$):
   $$\frac{\sin^2(\theta_i(m) - \theta_i(m_0))}{w_i^2} = \frac{(x - 1)^2(1 + u^2)}{x^2 + u^2} \ge \min\!\left(1, \frac{1}{x^2}\right)(x - 1)^2 = \rho(m, m_0)^2,$$
   hence $G_n(m) \ge \rho(m, m_0)^2 J_n$ **globally on all of $[0, \infty]$**.
2. **Non-Gaussian Compact-Interval Sharp Consistency:** Since $\sin^2(\delta_i(m))$ is monotone on each side of $m_0$ and $\sin(2\delta_i(m))$ is a difference of two monotone functions, on any compact $[\underline m, \overline m]$ containing $m_0$ the Lemma ($q = 2$) bounds $\sup_{m \in [\underline m, \overline m]} |Z_n(m)| = O_p(\sqrt{J_n + M_4 W_n})$ with **zero log factors and zero Gaussianity**, extending `B27`'s sharp $J_n^2 / (W_n + J_n) \to \infty$ to all finite-4th-moment non-Gaussian errors (and arbitrary correlated $k_i = 1$ SPD covariances).
3. **NEW COUNTEREXAMPLE — Global Ray Break of `B27`'s Local Weighted Criterion:** On the **unconstrained closed ray $[0, \infty]$** (`T81`), $J_n^2 / (W_n + J_n) \to \infty$ is **NOT sufficient**!
   - Take $m_0 = 1$: one reading at $s_1 = 1$ with $r_1^2 = 8\log N$, plus $N$ pure-noise Gaussian readings at $s_i = N$.
   - Local speeds at $m_0 = 1$: $w_1 = 1/2$, $w_i = N / (1 + N^2) < 1/N$ ($i \ge 2$), so $W_n = \frac14 + O(1/N)$ and $J_n = 2\log N \to \infty$, hence **`B27`'s $J_n^2 / (W_n + J_n) \asymp \log N \to \infty$** (compact accuracy on $[0.2, 5.0]$ climbs $0.867 \to 0.943 \to 1$).
   - Yet at the remote mass $m = N$, reading 1 pays at most $r_1^2 + O_p(r_1) = 8\log N$, while the $N$ pure-noise readings have $K_{\rm noise}(N) - K_{\rm noise}(1) \stackrel{d}{\approx} \mathcal N(0, 2N)$ (std dev $\sqrt{2N} \gg 8\log N$)! Therefore **$P\big(\arg\min_{[0,\infty]} K \ge N/2\big) \to \ge \frac12$** (`0.217 → 0.387 → 0.470` at $N = 100, 500, 2500$ in `tests/test_e17_l25_and_g25.py`).
4. **Global Sufficient Condition on $[0, \infty]$:** Applying the Lemma on all of $[0, \infty]$ gives $\sup_{m \in [0,\infty]} |Z_n(m)| = O_p(\sqrt{\Delta_n + M_4 D_n})$ uniformly in ALL $s_1, \dots, s_n \in (0, \infty)$! Combined with (1), $\inf_{\rho(m,m_0)\ge\epsilon} G_n(m) / \sqrt{M_4 D_n + \Delta_n} \to \infty$ (in particular $J_n / \sqrt{M_4 D_n + \Delta_n} \to \infty$) guarantees global consistency of `T81` on $[0, \infty]$.

### Theorem E17-G3 (Heavy-Tailed Finite-Covariance Errors $2 < \nu < 4$: Threshold Degradation & Link to Door B)

Suppose world errors have **exact finite covariance $\Sigma_i$** (`L18` holds!), uniform $\kappa(\Gamma_i) \le \kappa_0$, and finite $2q$-th moment $E[\|\varepsilon_i\|^{2q}] \le M_{2q} k_i^q$ for some $q \in (1, 2]$ (e.g., standardized Student-$t_\nu$ with $2 < \nu \le 4$ has finite $2q$-th moment for every $q < \nu/2$).

1. **Sufficiency (von Bahr–Esseen + Doob $L_q$):** The linear cross-term $2\mu_i^\top P_i'(\phi)\varepsilon_i$ needs only second moments and contributes $O_p(\sqrt{\Delta_n})$. For the quadratic noise term $\varepsilon_i^\top P_i'(\phi)\varepsilon_i$, von Bahr–Esseen (1965) gives
   $$E\!\left[\left|\sum_{i=1}^n \varepsilon_i^\top P_i'(\phi)\varepsilon_i\right|^q\right] \le 2\sum_{i=1}^n E\big[|\varepsilon_i^\top P_i'(\phi)\varepsilon_i|^q\big] = O(D_n) \implies \sup_{\phi \in [0,\pi/2]} |Z_n(\phi)| = O_p\!\big(D_n^{1/q} + \sqrt{\Delta_n}\big).$$
   Hence **`T81`'s global $\arg\min K$ is consistent whenever $\Delta_n / (D_n^{1/q} + \sqrt{\Delta_n}) \to \infty$** (at $t_\nu$: $\Delta_n / (D_n^{2/\nu + \epsilon} + \sqrt{\Delta_n}) \to \infty$; and at fixed $N$ as $\Delta \to \infty$, consistent for **every** tight error law, even Cauchy $\nu = 1$!).
2. **Sharp Converse (Breakdown of `T81` at `L11`'s Gaussian rate vs. Door B Recovery):** Under standardized Student-$t_\nu$ errors ($2 < \nu < 4$, exact $\operatorname{Cov} = I_2$), the maximum squared noise norm satisfies $\max_{i \le N} \|\varepsilon_i\|^2 \asymp_p N^{2/\nu}$. In the intermediate regime $N^{1/2} \ll \Delta_N \ll N^{2/\nu}$:
   - **`T81`'s quadratic $\arg\min K$ FAILS consistency:** a single extreme reading of squared norm $\sim N^{2/\nu} \gg \Delta_N$ dominates the entire series Gram matrix $G = \sum y_i y_i^\top$ and drags $\arg\min K$ to that outlier's random angle!
   - **Door B robust log-curve $R_c(m) = \sum_i \log(1 + K_i(m)/c)$ SUCCEEDS at $\Delta_N / \sqrt{N} \to \infty$:** by Anderson (1955) unimodality shift dominance, $E[R_c(m)]$ is uniquely minimized at $m_0$ for every spherical unimodal law (even Cauchy $\nu = 1$), and because $\log(1 + K_i/c)$ has bounded derivative and finite variance even with infinite 4th moment, $\arg\min R_c(m) \to m_0$ at the full $\Delta_N / \sqrt{N} \to \infty$ rate!
   - **Verified on standardized $t_{2.5}$ ($\operatorname{Cov} = I_2$ exact, $2/\nu = 0.8$) at $\Delta_N = 8 N^{0.65}$ (`tests/test_e17_l25_and_g25.py`):**
     - As $N$ grows `100 → 400 → 1600` ($\Delta_N/\sqrt{N}$ climbs `16.0 → 19.7 → 24.2`): **`T81` failure climbs `0.365 → 0.385 → 0.425`**, while **Door B log-curve failure drops `0.105 → 0.040 → 0.000`**!
     - Above `E17-G3`'s threshold ($\Delta_N = 6 N^{0.95} \gg N^{0.8}$), `T81` recovers consistency (`failure < 0.12` at $N = 800$).
