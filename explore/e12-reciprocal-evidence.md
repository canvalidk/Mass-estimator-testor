# From the reciprocal correction to a law over mass

**Two different amounts of information occur.** The gradient of the squared residual is the noise-metric dual of the common reciprocal correction. Integrating that particular differential gives the ordinary Gaussian evidence uniquely. More generally, *no* positive decreasing function applied to the total cost at each candidate can both retain spread on a fixed incompatible series and concentrate the law on every identifying series. The counterexample even permits an explicit dependence on the number of readings when exactly zero measured rows are neutral. Thus changing only the shape of the response to a candidate's total residual cannot finish this proof.

## The differential that geometry supplies

Fix the prior and write the relative data factor as \(e_D(m)>0\). In any smooth mass coordinate \(x\), variation of the graph gives \(s_i=-\partial_x K_i/2\), with \(s_i=h_i b_i\) for each nonzero tangent. The common reciprocal correction is \(\bar b=\sum s_i/H\), \(H=\sum h_i\). If the change of log evidence along a displacement is required to equal its unmodified noise-metric pairing with that correction, then
\[
 d\log e_D=H\bar b\,dx=-\tfrac12\,dK_D,\qquad K_D=\sum_i K_i .
\]
Use \(\sum s_i=0\) at zero common tangent. This is a one-form, independent of the chart: the tangent metric and signed displacement transform with compensating Jacobians. Integration on the connected positive mass domain fixes \(e_D\propto\exp(-K_D/2)\). This recovers the familiar undamped mass evidence by a reciprocal construction. Equating *log evidence* with this geometrical work is a named additional step; the geometry alone gives the right-hand side. On the colliding experiments from E12, the two cost curves differ by a constant, so this entire one-form and its integrated relative evidence agree. ## A dichotomy for every monotone response to absolute cost

Consider \(P_D(d\theta)\propto\pi(d\theta) f(K_D(\theta))\), with \(f\) finite, strictly positive and nonincreasing on \([0,\infty)\), a common standardized scalar instrument, and a fixed prior of full support with positive continuous angular density. No differentiability or tail expansion is assumed. Use the same standardized function through noise shrinkage and across the repeated-push experiments below, subject to the count extension at the end. A fixed function chosen using the dimensionless declarations and prior is allowed. The rule sees a candidate's *absolute* cost, so the previous normalized-curve collision does not by itself rule it out. No other statistic of this observed experiment enters \(f\).

Use the fixed incompatible readings \((4,1),(1,4)\) in reference noise units. They have cost
\[
 k(\theta)=9+16\sin^2(\theta-\pi/4)
\]
and minimum \(a=9\) at the unique interior line \(\hat\theta=\pi/4\). Shrinking both errors multiplies \(k\) by \(t\to\infty\) at a fixed noise ratio. The project asks this fixed conflicting series to retain spread, for instance \(\liminf_{t\to\infty}\operatorname{Var}_{P_t}(\theta)>0\). A subsequence concentrating on \(\hat\theta\) violates even this weak form. [Whole-series reading of T30](../definition/theorems/T30.md)

**If the tail ratios \(f(x)/f(2x)\) are unbounded along \(x\to\infty\)**, set \(h_j=2^{1/j}\). For each fixed \(j\), the ratios \(f(x)/f(h_jx)\) are unbounded arbitrarily far out, since multiplying \(j\) successive such ratios gives a doubling ratio. The set \(A_j=\{k\le h_j a\}\) has positive prior mass. Choose \(x_j\to\infty\) with \(f(x_j)/f(h_j x_j)>j/\pi(A_j)\) and set \(t_j=x_j/(h_j a)\). On \(A_j\) the factor is at least \(f(x_j)\). On \(\{k\ge h_j^2 a\}\) it is at most \(f(h_jx_j)\). Thus
\[
 P_{t_j}\{k\ge h_j^2a\}<1/j .
\]
The sublevel neighborhoods shrink to the unique minimizing line. The angle variance tends to zero along this subsequence because the arc is bounded. This proves loss of spread even for a discontinuous monotone response with arbitrarily long plateaus.

**If the tail ratios are eventually bounded** by \(M\), take independent repeated Gaussian readings with one fixed nonzero signed push \(r\) and an interior true angle \(\theta_0\). Their scatter divided by \(N\) converges almost surely to \(\mathrm{Id}+r^2 u_0u_0^\top\). Hence, eventually almost surely, *all* candidate costs lie between \(N/2\) and \(2N(1+r^2)\). Their largest-to-smallest factor ratio is bounded by \(M^j\), with the same fixed integer \(j=\lceil\log_2(4(1+r^2))\rceil\) for every \(N\). Every angular set of fixed positive prior mass retains at least \(M^{-j}\) times that mass. Thus the law fails to concentrate at the true mass even though information grows linearly in \(N\). These two cases exhaust the class. This is a statement about the returned *law*. An accurate point alone is a weaker requirement. [Scalar information threshold](../definition/theorems/T53.md)

**Why adding a declared count alone cannot evade this argument under zero neutrality.** Allow a different positive response \(f_n\) for every count \(n\), holding the other declarations fixed. Appending an exactly measured zero leaves \(K\) unchanged. For any \(0<u<v\), two scalar rows on the coordinate axes can have scatter \(\operatorname{diag}(u,v)\). Their candidate costs cover the entire interval \((u,v)\). Pad with zeros to reach any \(n\ge2\). Neutrality of the normalized law then requires \(f_{n+1}/f_n\) constant almost everywhere on \((u,v)\). Overlapping intervals force a single global proportionality almost everywhere for positive costs. Monotonicity makes any remaining differences lie among countably many discontinuity points. The conflicting example and almost every Gaussian experiment above have nonconstant trigonometric cost, so these exceptional levels have zero prior measure. The same dichotomy follows. An observed effective count, the cost relative to its minimum, or separately retaining the tangent disagreement accesses extra information and is outside the theorem.

The geometric question this leaves is specific. The ordinary differential \(H\bar b\,dx\) only measures preference between neighboring masses. The orthogonal residuals measure incompatibility even at \(\bar b=0\). A mass law satisfying both requirements needs a rule carrying information beyond the single number \(K_D(\theta)\) into its relative evidence. The tangent construction supplies such information and a uniquely selected local conflict diagnostic under an explicit decision criterion.
