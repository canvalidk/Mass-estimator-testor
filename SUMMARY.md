## Seven numbered theorems / findings (for Can & Claude to harvest)

1. **`E14-1` — Tropical uniqueness of $\arg\min_{m>0} K(m)$ on $\overline{\mathcal K}_{\rm int}$** (`B28`, `G6`, `O2`, `L5`, `L14`, `L3`, `L7`): iterating scaled `T74` infimal convolutions $Q_n = (K_+^{(n)})_\parallel^{(n)}$ preserves $R(Q_n)=R(K)$ by `L5`+`L14`+`L3` and pinches **both** sides of $m^*$ to $+\infty$ at rate $\ge \frac{\epsilon c(\epsilon/2)}{2(m^*+\epsilon)} n$, forcing $R(K)=\arg\min K$ uniquely under exact-branch concentration. Inverts the 28 Oct Jensen conflict: on bounded `T73` curves, `L5` pinches only $m<m^*$ and `L14` pinches only $m>m^*$; both are required and cooperate.
2. **`E14-2` — Boundary obstruction on $(0,\infty)$ vs. exact closure on $[0,\infty]$** (`B28`, `L2`, `L5`, `L14`, `T80`, `R-2026-11-22-2`): no extension $R_{\rm ext}:\mathcal K_{\rm all}\to(0,\infty)$ agreeing with $\arg\min K$ on $\mathcal K_{\rm int}$ can preserve `L14` on $[L,\infty)$ curves or `L5` on $(0,U]$ curves (4-line proof: $K_{\rm int}\square_\parallel K_{\rm inf}\in\mathcal K_{\rm int}$ has unique interior minimum $m_1^*$, forcing $R_{\rm ext}(K_{\rm inf})=\infty$). Full 4-way taxonomy of non-unique `T73` curves (flat curves including non-zero orthogonal scatter `{(1,0),(0,1)}`, one-sided boundary minima, anti-aligned $\{0,\infty\}$ ties, explicit 2-instrument interior bimodal tie).
3. **`E14-3` — $K(m)$ alone is insufficient past one instrument; $(K, C)$ is minimal** (`T77 (4)`, `T45`, `O20`): resolves `T77.md` line 104 negatively. Two series on instruments $s_1=1$ and $s_2=2$ have bit-for-bit identical $K_A(m)\equiv K_B(m)\equiv 20$ yet $C_A(1)=5.0$ vs. $C_B(1)=3.2$, giving opposite 95% `T45` verdicts (`3.79` kept vs. `4.68` rejected) when combined with the same reading. Since $S(m)=-\frac12 K'(m)$ identically on the full correlated-$\Sigma_i$, subspace-$N_i$ type, $(K(m), C(m))$ is the exact minimal additive state.
4. **`E14-4` — Exact curve-pair two-body composition `Q_pos` with zero Monte Carlo** (`T74 (5)–(6)`, `L5`, `L14`): positive-quadrant profiled score $Q_{\rm pos}(m_1; M)$ depends **only** on $(K_1, C_1)$ and $(K_2, C_2)$ (no raw readings, no counts, deterministic 1D Gauss–Legendre survival law). Matches exact 1-dof strong-signal sharpness (`0.0217` at 2.3$\sigma$ vs. `0.02145`), exact Chernoff one-sided bound (`4.18` on `5±0.5 + 0/0`), exact Shapiro $\bar\chi^2$ corner law, exact `L14` swap duality, and $\ge 95\%$ weak-signal 3D coverage (`0.95–0.97` where raw $K_{\rm lump}$ gets `0.85` and naive compressed $(K_{\rm lump}, C_{\rm lump})$ collapses to `0.620`/`0.785`).
5. **`E14-5` — Collapse of `T76`'s weighted-score continuum to $w\equiv 1$ (`T45`)** (`T76`, `F2`, `L17`, `O20`): inside `T76`, $w\equiv 1$ is uniquely selected by keeping $\arg\min K$ inside the confidence set (explicit counterexamples where both $w=H/(1+H)$ and $w=H$ reject $\arg\min K$ at $p=0.0027$ and $p<10^{-35}$), by `L17` curve sufficiency on one instrument, or by curve-pair composability $S_w=-\frac12 K'$.
6. **`E14-6` — Curve-only discount no-go theorem** (`T30`, `R-2026-11-17-3`, `L11`, `L17`, `L20`): no rule $S(K, C)$ can satisfy both `T30` (bounded confidence on `(5, 6)` as $\sigma\to 0$) and `L11` ($O(1/\sqrt N)$ set width on $N$ honest readings at $\sigma=0.5$), because 2 conflicting readings at $\sigma_A=\Delta/\sqrt{2(N-1)}$ and $N$ honest zero-surprise readings at $\sigma_B=\frac\Delta 2\sqrt{N/(N-1)}$ produce bit-for-bit identical $(K, C)$. Any disagreement discount therefore requires an effective reading count ($M_{\rm nz}$: exact `Z`, discontinuous at `0/0`; or smooth $\sum h_b(\|z_i\|^2)$: uncountable continuum, breaks `L17`).
7. **`E14-7` — Exact closed-form joint-spherical score floor `T45` $\cup$ `t_joint` on $(K, C, M_{\rm nz})$** (`O14`, `O20`, `T75`, `T78`): $t_{\rm joint}(m)=\sqrt{M_{\rm nz}-1}\,S/\sqrt{KC-S^2}\sim t_{M_{\rm nz}-1}$ is exact on the full correlated-$\Sigma_i$, subspace-$N_i$ type under any unknown common scale $v>0$ (zero MC), uniquely pins `O14`'s "expected over observed leftover, capped at 1" to exponent **1**, converges to **exact 95.0%** on honest Gaussian series as $N\to\infty$ (`0.997→0.953`), gives `5.55±0.138` near / `5.50±0.574` far ($\sigma$-invariant) in 3D, and gives the unique equivariant finite $t_1$ set on 2 scalar readings (`±0.63` near, `±6.35` far, where `T75 (6)` is vacuous for $n\le 5$). **Honest breaks:** discontinuous at `0/0`, $df=0$ at $M_{\rm nz}=1$, keeps score hilltop/right-angle ghosts, under-covers per-reading $\nu=2$ heavy tails (`0.86` vs `T75 (6)` `0.94`).
